*# 1. Purpose*



*This document defines the official Context Lifecycle governing the Francisco Sánchez Portfolio AI-First engineering ecosystem.*



*The Context Lifecycle specifies how engineering context is created, validated, published, consumed, maintained, evolved, archived, and retired throughout its entire existence.*



*Context shall be treated as a living engineering asset.*



*---*



*# 2. Objectives*



*The Context Lifecycle aims to:*



*- Standardize context evolution.*

*- Preserve engineering continuity.*

*- Prevent obsolete information.*

*- Maintain repository integrity.*

*- Improve AI reliability.*

*- Support long-term governance.*

*- Enable continuous context evolution.*

*- Reduce engineering entropy.*



*---*



*# 3. Lifecycle Philosophy*



*Engineering context is never static.*



*Every context artifact shall evolve under governance while preserving historical traceability.*



*Lifecycle management shall prioritize consistency over convenience.*



*---*



*# 4. Scope*



*The Context Lifecycle applies to:*



*- Engineering Standards.*

*- Architecture.*

*- ADRs.*

*- Repository Documentation.*

*- AI Memory.*

*- Knowledge Base.*

*- Workflows.*

*- Templates.*

*- Governance Documents.*

*- Quality Documentation.*



*Every managed context artifact follows this lifecycle.*



*---*



*# 5. Lifecycle States*



*Every context artifact shall exist in one of the following states:*



*- Draft*

*- Validation*

*- Approved*

*- Active*

*- Updated*

*- Deprecated*

*- Archived*



*State transitions shall remain fully traceable.*



*---*



*# 6. Lifecycle Flow*



*```*

*Draft*



*↓*



*Validation*



*↓*



*Approval*



*↓*



*Publication*



*↓*



*Active Usage*



*↓*



*Continuous Review*



*↓*



*Update*



*↓*



*Deprecation*



*↓*



*Archive*

*```*



*No lifecycle stage shall be skipped.*



*---*



*# 7. Draft Phase*



*During Draft:*



*- Content is authored.*

*- Metadata is created.*

*- Relationships are established.*

*- Ownership is assigned.*



*Draft artifacts shall not be considered authoritative.*



*---*



*# 8. Validation Phase*



*Validation verifies:*



*- Structure.*

*- Accuracy.*

*- Governance compliance.*

*- References.*

*- Consistency.*

*- Completeness.*



*Only validated artifacts may proceed to approval.*



*---*



*# 9. Approval Phase*



*Approval confirms that the artifact:*



*- Meets engineering standards.*

*- Satisfies governance.*

*- Is internally consistent.*

*- Is suitable for publication.*



*Approval is mandatory before activation.*



*---*



*# 10. Active Phase*



*Active artifacts:*



*- Become authoritative.*

*- May be referenced by AI.*

*- Participate in engineering workflows.*

*- Serve as official repository knowledge.*



*Only Active artifacts shall guide engineering decisions.*



*---*



*# 11. Continuous Review*



*Active artifacts shall undergo periodic review to verify:*



*- Technical accuracy.*

*- Organizational relevance.*

*- Governance alignment.*

*- Architectural compatibility.*

*- Repository consistency.*



*Review frequency shall follow governance policy.*



*---*



*# 12. Update Phase*



*Updates may occur due to:*



*- Architecture evolution.*

*- Governance changes.*

*- Engineering improvements.*

*- Repository restructuring.*

*- Operational experience.*

*- AI recommendations.*



*Updates shall preserve version history.*



*---*



*# 13. Deprecation Phase*



*Artifacts become Deprecated when:*



*- Replaced by newer standards.*

*- No longer technically valid.*

*- Superseded by governance.*

*- Retired from engineering practice.*



*Deprecated artifacts remain accessible for historical reference.*



*---*



*# 14. Archive Phase*



*Archived artifacts:*



*- Preserve organizational history.*

*- Remain immutable.*

*- Are excluded from active reasoning.*

*- Support audits and traceability.*



*Archived context shall never be deleted without governance approval.*



*---*



*# 15. Version Evolution*



*Lifecycle progression shall preserve:*



*- Semantic Versioning.*

*- Revision history.*

*- Approval history.*

*- Change rationale.*

*- Historical references.*



*Version history shall remain permanent.*



*---*



*# 16. AI Integration*



*AI systems shall:*



*- Respect lifecycle states.*

*- Consume only Active artifacts.*

*- Ignore Deprecated artifacts unless explicitly requested.*

*- Preserve historical traceability.*

*- Recommend lifecycle updates.*



*Lifecycle awareness is mandatory for AI reasoning.*



*---*



*# 17. Lifecycle Monitoring*



*Recommended lifecycle indicators include:*



*- Active Context Count*

*- Deprecated Context Count*

*- Average Context Age*

*- Review Compliance Rate*

*- Update Frequency*

*- Archive Growth*

*- Lifecycle Completion Rate*



*These metrics support continuous optimization.*



*---*



*# 18. AI Operational Contract*



*## Responsibilities*



*AI systems shall:*



*- Respect lifecycle states.*

*- Detect obsolete context.*

*- Recommend updates.*

*- Preserve historical continuity.*

*- Avoid reasoning from deprecated information.*



*---*



*## Authorized Actions*



*AI may:*



*- Classify lifecycle state.*

*- Recommend reviews.*

*- Suggest updates.*

*- Detect stale documentation.*

*- Produce lifecycle reports.*



*---*



*## Restricted Actions*



*AI shall not:*



*- Publish artifacts autonomously.*

*- Delete archived context.*

*- Override approval workflows.*

*- Promote draft documentation to active status.*



*---*



*## Success Criteria*



*The Context Lifecycle is successful when:*



*- Repository knowledge remains current.*

*- Context evolves continuously.*

*- Historical traceability is preserved.*

*- AI consumes only valid context.*

*- Engineering documentation remains healthy.*



*---*



*## Failure Conditions*



*The Context Lifecycle fails when:*



*- Obsolete context remains active.*

*- Active documentation lacks review.*

*- Historical information is lost.*

*- AI reasons from deprecated artifacts.*

*- Lifecycle stages are bypassed.*



*---*



*## Escalation*



*Critical lifecycle failures affecting governance, architecture, repository integrity, AI reasoning, documentation health, or engineering continuity shall require immediate review by the Project Owner.*



*---*



*# 19. Governance*



*The Context Lifecycle governs every transition experienced by engineering context throughout the AI-First engineering ecosystem.*



*All future documentation systems, repository management tools, AI memory engines, semantic repositories, governance platforms, and engineering assistants shall comply with this lifecycle.*
