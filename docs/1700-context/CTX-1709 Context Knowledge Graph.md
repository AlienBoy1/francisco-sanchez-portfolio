*# 1. Purpose*



*This document defines the official Context Knowledge Graph governing the Francisco Sánchez Portfolio AI-First engineering ecosystem.*



*The Context Knowledge Graph specifies how engineering knowledge shall be represented as a connected semantic graph, allowing humans, AI agents, automation systems, and future engineering services to understand relationships between engineering artifacts beyond simple document retrieval.*



*The Knowledge Graph becomes the semantic representation of the engineering ecosystem.*



*---*



*# 2. Objectives*



*The Context Knowledge Graph aims to:*



*- Represent engineering knowledge semantically.*

*- Improve AI reasoning.*

*- Eliminate isolated documentation.*

*- Enable relationship-based retrieval.*

*- Improve traceability.*

*- Support dependency analysis.*

*- Enable impact analysis.*

*- Preserve organizational intelligence.*



*---*



*# 3. Knowledge Graph Philosophy*



*Engineering knowledge is not a collection of documents.*



*Engineering knowledge is a network of relationships.*



*Every artifact exists because it relates to other artifacts.*



*Understanding relationships is as important as understanding content.*



*---*



*# 4. Scope*



*The Knowledge Graph includes:*



*- Governance*

*- Engineering Standards*

*- Architecture*

*- ADRs*

*- Repository Structure*

*- Features*

*- Workflows*

*- Templates*

*- Quality Standards*

*- AI Agents*

*- Prompts*

*- Documentation*

*- Metrics*

*- Knowledge Base*

*- AI Memory*



*Every engineering artifact becomes a graph node.*



*---*



*# 5. Graph Principles*



*The Knowledge Graph shall be:*



*- Deterministic.*

*- Authoritative.*

*- Version-aware.*

*- Governed.*

*- Traceable.*

*- Searchable.*

*- Extensible.*



*---*



*# 6. Graph Model*



*The graph consists of:*



*- Nodes*

*- Relationships*

*- Metadata*

*- Labels*

*- Properties*

*- Versions*



*Every node shall have a globally unique identifier.*



*---*



*# 7. Node Types*



*Typical node categories include:*



*- Governance*

*- Standard*

*- Architecture*

*- ADR*

*- Workflow*

*- Template*

*- Feature*

*- Component*

*- Repository*

*- Module*

*- AI Agent*

*- Prompt*

*- Knowledge*

*- Metric*



*New node types may be introduced through governance.*



*---*



*# 8. Relationship Types*



*Supported relationship examples include:*



*- Depends On*

*- References*

*- Extends*

*- Implements*

*- Uses*

*- Generates*

*- Owns*

*- Replaces*

*- Belongs To*

*- Requires*

*- Validates*

*- Synchronizes With*

*- Related To*



*Relationships are first-class engineering assets.*



*---*



*# 9. Relationship Integrity*



*Every relationship shall preserve:*



*- Source node*

*- Target node*

*- Relationship type*

*- Version compatibility*

*- Lifecycle compatibility*

*- Traceability*



*Broken relationships shall be treated as repository integrity failures.*



*---*



*# 10. Semantic Navigation*



*The graph shall support navigation by:*



*- Dependency*

*- Ownership*

*- Architecture*

*- Governance*

*- Workflow*

*- Repository*

*- AI Agent*

*- Engineering Domain*



*Navigation shall not depend solely on text search.*



*---*



*# 11. Dependency Analysis*



*The Knowledge Graph shall support:*



*- Upstream dependency analysis.*

*- Downstream dependency analysis.*

*- Circular dependency detection.*

*- Impact analysis.*

*- Repository evolution planning.*



*Dependency analysis shall remain deterministic.*



*---*



*# 12. Impact Analysis*



*Before modifying any engineering artifact, the ecosystem shall be capable of identifying:*



*- Direct dependencies.*

*- Indirect dependencies.*

*- Affected documentation.*

*- Affected workflows.*

*- Affected standards.*

*- Affected AI agents.*

*- Affected repository modules.*



*The graph shall enable safe engineering evolution.*



*---*



*# 13. AI Integration*



*AI systems shall:*



*- Navigate the graph.*

*- Discover relationships.*

*- Explain dependency chains.*

*- Recommend missing context.*

*- Perform semantic reasoning.*



*Graph traversal shall complement document retrieval.*



*---*



*# 14. Evolution*



*The Knowledge Graph shall evolve continuously as:*



*- New documents appear.*

*- Relationships change.*

*- Architecture evolves.*

*- Governance expands.*

*- Repository structure changes.*



*Graph evolution shall preserve history.*



*---*



*# 15. Synchronization*



*Graph relationships shall remain synchronized with:*



*- Repository documentation.*

*- Architecture.*

*- Governance.*

*- Workflows.*

*- AI Memory.*

*- Knowledge Base.*



*Graph consistency is mandatory.*



*---*



*# 16. Performance*



*The Knowledge Graph shall optimize:*



*- Relationship traversal.*

*- Dependency analysis.*

*- Context discovery.*

*- Semantic retrieval.*

*- AI reasoning efficiency.*



*Performance shall never compromise correctness.*



*---*



*# 17. Metrics*



*Recommended metrics include:*



*- Node Count*

*- Relationship Count*

*- Graph Density*

*- Broken Relationships*

*- Dependency Depth*

*- Average Traversal Cost*

*- Graph Consistency Index*



*These metrics support continuous improvement.*



*---*



*# 18. AI Operational Contract*



*## Responsibilities*



*AI systems shall:*



*- Respect graph authority.*

*- Preserve semantic integrity.*

*- Use relationships during reasoning.*

*- Detect missing relationships.*

*- Recommend graph improvements.*



*---*



*## Authorized Actions*



*AI may:*



*- Traverse the graph.*

*- Query relationships.*

*- Recommend new edges.*

*- Detect orphan nodes.*

*- Produce dependency reports.*



*---*



*## Restricted Actions*



*AI shall not:*



*- Create authoritative relationships without approval.*

*- Remove governed relationships.*

*- Ignore graph integrity.*

*- Invent semantic connections.*

*- Override governance-defined dependencies.*



*---*



*## Success Criteria*



*The Context Knowledge Graph is successful when:*



*- Engineering knowledge becomes fully connected.*

*- AI reasoning improves through semantic navigation.*

*- Dependency analysis becomes deterministic.*

*- Repository evolution becomes safer.*

*- Organizational knowledge becomes explorable.*



*---*



*## Failure Conditions*



*The Context Knowledge Graph fails when:*



*- Relationships become inconsistent.*

*- Nodes become orphaned.*

*- Dependency analysis becomes unreliable.*

*- AI ignores semantic relationships.*

*- Repository evolution loses traceability.*



*---*



*## Escalation*



*Critical graph integrity failures affecting governance, architecture, repository consistency, AI reasoning, dependency analysis, or organizational knowledge shall require immediate review by the Project Owner.*



*---*



*# 19. Governance*



*The Context Knowledge Graph governs the semantic representation of every engineering artifact within the AI-First engineering ecosystem.*



*All future graph databases, semantic repositories, vector databases, ontology engines, AI memory platforms, repository intelligence systems, and engineering assistants shall comply with this standard.*
