*# 1. Purpose*



*This document establishes the foundational principles governing Context Management throughout the Francisco Sánchez Portfolio AI-First engineering ecosystem.*



*Context Management defines how engineering knowledge, operational information, architectural decisions, project state, historical records, AI memory, and repository intelligence are acquired, organized, validated, maintained, synchronized, and consumed by both humans and AI systems.*



*Within an AI-First ecosystem, context is considered a strategic engineering asset.*



*---*



*# 2. Objectives*



*The Context Management Foundation aims to:*



*- Standardize context handling.*

*- Preserve engineering continuity.*

*- Improve AI decision quality.*

*- Reduce information fragmentation.*

*- Minimize context loss.*

*- Enable consistent engineering decisions.*

*- Support long-term organizational memory.*

*- Increase engineering productivity.*



*---*



*# 3. Context Philosophy*



*Engineering decisions are only as good as the context available.*



*Context shall be:*



*- Accurate.*

*- Complete.*

*- Relevant.*

*- Traceable.*

*- Version controlled.*

*- Continuously synchronized.*

*- Governed.*



*Missing context represents engineering risk.*



*---*



*# 4. Scope*



*Context Management applies to:*



*- Engineering standards.*

*- Architecture.*

*- Documentation.*

*- AI prompts.*

*- AI agents.*

*- Source code.*

*- Infrastructure.*

*- Workflows.*

*- Governance.*

*- Quality.*

*- Operational knowledge.*

*- Repository state.*



*Every engineering activity consumes context.*



*---*



*# 5. Definition of Context*



*Within this ecosystem, context represents every piece of information required to correctly understand, execute, validate, or evolve an engineering activity.*



*Context may include:*



*- Documents.*

*- ADRs.*

*- Standards.*

*- Repository structure.*

*- Historical decisions.*

*- AI memory.*

*- Metrics.*

*- Workflows.*

*- Technical constraints.*

*- Business rules.*

*- Operational status.*



*---*



*# 6. Context Principles*



*Context shall always be:*



*- Explicit.*

*- Discoverable.*

*- Reusable.*

*- Structured.*

*- Validated.*

*- Continuously maintained.*

*- Independent of individuals.*



*Context shall never depend solely on human memory.*



*---*



*# 7. Context Layers*



*The ecosystem organizes context into multiple layers:*



*- Strategic Context*

*- Organizational Context*

*- Engineering Context*

*- Project Context*

*- Repository Context*

*- Operational Context*

*- AI Context*

*- Session Context*

*- Task Context*



*Each layer contributes different levels of information.*



*---*



*# 8. Context Hierarchy*



*Context flows hierarchically:*



*```*

*Organization*



*↓*



*Governance*



*↓*



*Engineering Standards*



*↓*



*Architecture*



*↓*



*Project*



*↓*



*Repository*



*↓*



*Feature*



*↓*



*Task*



*↓*



*Session*

*```*



*Higher-level context always has precedence over lower-level assumptions.*



*---*



*# 9. Context Sources*



*Authorized context sources include:*



*- Repository documentation.*

*- Engineering standards.*

*- ADRs.*

*- Workflows.*

*- Governance.*

*- Quality reports.*

*- Architecture documentation.*

*- AI memory.*

*- Historical decisions.*

*- Operational metrics.*



*External information shall be validated before becoming official context.*



*---*



*# 10. Context Consumers*



*Context may be consumed by:*



*- Engineers.*

*- AI agents.*

*- Automation systems.*

*- CI/CD pipelines.*

*- Governance processes.*

*- Quality systems.*

*- Operational workflows.*



*Every consumer shall follow the same governance rules.*



*---*



*# 11. Context Quality*



*High-quality context shall be:*



*- Correct.*

*- Current.*

*- Complete.*

*- Consistent.*

*- Relevant.*

*- Non-duplicated.*



*Low-quality context increases engineering risk.*



*---*



*# 12. Context Evolution*



*Context continuously evolves through:*



*- Engineering changes.*

*- Architecture evolution.*

*- Repository updates.*

*- AI learning.*

*- Governance decisions.*

*- Continuous improvement.*

*- Operational experience.*



*Context shall evolve without losing historical traceability.*



*---*



*# 13. AI Integration*



*AI systems shall:*



*- Load context before reasoning.*

*- Preserve context integrity.*

*- Detect missing context.*

*- Recommend additional context.*

*- Avoid hallucinations caused by insufficient context.*



*Context loading is mandatory before decision-making.*



*---*



*# 14. Engineering Responsibilities*



*Every engineer is responsible for:*



*- Maintaining context.*

*- Updating documentation.*

*- Preserving traceability.*

*- Preventing information loss.*

*- Synchronizing repository knowledge.*



*Context ownership is shared across the organization.*



*---*



*# 15. Success Indicators*



*The Context Management Foundation is successful when:*



*- Engineering decisions remain consistent.*

*- AI responses improve over time.*

*- Repository knowledge remains synchronized.*

*- Information retrieval becomes efficient.*

*- Organizational memory grows continuously.*

*- Context loss approaches zero.*



*---*



*# 16. AI Operational Contract*



*## Responsibilities*



*AI systems shall:*



*- Load relevant context.*

*- Validate context consistency.*

*- Detect missing information.*

*- Recommend documentation updates.*

*- Preserve engineering continuity.*



*---*



*## Authorized Actions*



*AI may:*



*- Retrieve context.*

*- Organize context.*

*- Summarize context.*

*- Recommend related artifacts.*

*- Detect inconsistencies.*



*---*



*## Restricted Actions*



*AI shall not:*



*- Invent context.*

*- Ignore governance.*

*- Replace official documentation.*

*- Remove historical context.*

*- Override validated engineering knowledge.*



*---*



*## Success Criteria*



*The Context Management Foundation is successful when AI consistently reasons using validated organizational context rather than isolated conversation history.*



*---*



*## Failure Conditions*



*The Context Management Foundation fails when:*



*- Decisions are made using incomplete information.*

*- Repository knowledge diverges.*

*- Historical decisions become unrecoverable.*

*- AI hallucinates due to missing context.*

*- Organizational knowledge becomes fragmented.*



*---*



*## Escalation*



*Any detected loss of critical engineering context involving governance, architecture, quality, repository integrity, AI memory, or organizational knowledge shall be escalated immediately to the Project Owner.*



*---*



*# 17. Governance*



*The Context Management Foundation governs every mechanism responsible for acquiring, validating, organizing, storing, synchronizing, and consuming engineering context throughout the AI-First engineering ecosystem.*



*All future context architectures, AI memory systems, repository indexing mechanisms, knowledge graphs, context synchronization processes, and engineering assistants shall comply with this standard.*
