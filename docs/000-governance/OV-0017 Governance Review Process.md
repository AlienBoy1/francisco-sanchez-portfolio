*# 1. Purpose*



*This document defines the Governance Review Process for the Francisco Sánchez Portfolio project.*



*Its purpose is to ensure that governance documents, engineering standards, policies, and processes remain accurate, relevant, effective, and aligned with the project's evolution.*



*Governance shall be treated as a living system that evolves together with the software it governs.*



*---*



*# 2. Objectives*



*This document aims to:*



*- Maintain governance accuracy.*

*- Detect outdated standards.*

*- Improve engineering practices.*

*- Verify compliance.*

*- Encourage continuous improvement.*

*- Support long-term maintainability.*

*- Preserve consistency across all governance artifacts.*



*---*



*# 3. Governance Philosophy*



*Governance is never considered finished.*



*Every standard shall be periodically reviewed to ensure that it continues to represent the project's current engineering practices.*



*Continuous review prevents documentation decay and reduces governance debt.*



*---*



*# 4. Review Scope*



*Governance reviews shall evaluate:*



*- Governance standards.*

*- Repository organization.*

*- Engineering policies.*

*- Documentation quality.*

*- Architecture decisions.*

*- Development workflows.*

*- Quality assurance practices.*

*- Release process.*

*- Risk management.*

*- Compliance status.*



*---*



*# 5. Review Triggers*



*Governance reviews should occur under one or more of the following conditions:*



*- Scheduled periodic review.*

*- Major release.*

*- Significant architectural change.*

*- Framework migration.*

*- Introduction of new governance documents.*

*- Lessons learned from incidents.*

*- Repository restructuring.*



*Additional reviews may be performed whenever necessary.*



*---*



*# 6. Review Frequency*



*Governance shall be reviewed at least:*



*- Quarterly.*

*- Before every major release.*

*- After major architectural changes.*

*- Following significant production incidents.*



*Critical governance issues should be reviewed immediately.*



*---*



*# 7. Review Responsibilities*



*The Project Owner is responsible for initiating governance reviews.*



*The Technical Lead is responsible for evaluating:*



*- Technical consistency.*

*- Architectural alignment.*

*- Engineering quality.*



*The Software Engineer is responsible for:*



*- Updating affected documentation.*

*- Implementing approved improvements.*

*- Correcting identified non-conformities.*



*---*



*# 8. Review Criteria*



*Each review should evaluate:*



*- Accuracy.*

*- Completeness.*

*- Consistency.*

*- Traceability.*

*- Relevance.*

*- Maintainability.*

*- Compliance with project standards.*



*Reviews should focus on actionable improvements rather than administrative completeness.*



*---*



*# 9. Review Outcomes*



*Each review shall produce one or more of the following outcomes:*



*- Approved without changes.*

*- Approved with recommendations.*

*- Requires revisions.*

*- Superseded.*

*- Deprecated.*



*The review outcome shall be documented.*



*---*



*# 10. Governance Improvements*



*Improvement opportunities identified during reviews shall be documented and prioritized.*



*Improvements may include:*



*- New standards.*

*- Updated policies.*

*- Repository restructuring.*

*- Documentation enhancements.*

*- Process optimization.*

*- Automation opportunities.*



*Continuous improvement is an expected result of every review.*



*---*



*# 11. Documentation Updates*



*Approved governance changes shall be reflected in all affected documentation.*



*Version history shall record:*



*- Date.*

*- Author.*

*- Summary of changes.*

*- Version number.*



*Documentation shall remain synchronized with approved governance decisions.*



*---*



*# 12. Governance Metrics*



*Governance reviews should consider the metrics defined in GOV-0016.*



*Metrics shall be used to:*



*- Detect trends.*

*- Identify recurring issues.*

*- Evaluate improvement effectiveness.*

*- Support evidence-based governance decisions.*



*Metrics shall complement, not replace, engineering judgment.*



*---*



*# 13. Continuous Evolution*



*Governance is expected to evolve alongside the project.*



*Standards that no longer provide value should be revised, consolidated, or retired.*



*Evolution shall prioritize clarity, simplicity, and engineering effectiveness.*



*---*



*# 14. Compliance*



*Governance reviews are mandatory.*



*Failure to perform periodic reviews increases governance debt and reduces long-term maintainability.*



*Review findings shall be documented and addressed according to project priorities.*
