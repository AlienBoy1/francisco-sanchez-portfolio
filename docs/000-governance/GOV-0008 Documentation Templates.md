*# 1. Purpose*



*This document establishes the official documentation templates used throughout the Francisco Sánchez Portfolio project.*



*The objective is to ensure that every engineering document follows a consistent structure, facilitating readability, maintainability, navigation, traceability, and long-term scalability.*



*Every document created within this repository shall originate from one of the templates defined in this standard.*



*---*



*# 2. Objectives*



*This document aims to:*



*- Standardize document structure.*

*- Reduce documentation inconsistencies.*

*- Simplify document creation.*

*- Improve maintainability.*

*- Facilitate navigation.*

*- Preserve engineering quality.*

*- Promote documentation-first development.*



*---*



*# 3. Documentation Philosophy*



*Templates are engineering assets.*



*They define structure, not content.*



*Using standardized templates ensures consistency across all engineering domains while allowing each document to focus on its specific purpose.*



*No document should be created from scratch.*



*---*



*# 4. Standard Document Structure*



*Every engineering document shall include the following sections unless explicitly exempted.*



*```text*

*Title*



*Metadata*



*Purpose*



*Objectives*



*Main Content*



*References*



*Related Documents*



*Revision History*



*Approval*

*```*



*Additional sections may be introduced when required by the document type.*



*---*

*Every document shall begin with the following metadata.*



*```markdown*

*# Metadata*



*| Field | Value |*

*|--------|-------|*

*| Title | Document Title |*

*| Version | 1.0.0 |*

*| Owner | Francisco Javier Sánchez Mariscal |*

*| Role | Software Engineer |*

*| Approver | Project Owner |*

*| Last Updated | YYYY-MM-DD |*

*```*



*Metadata fields shall remain synchronized with the current document version.*



*---*



*# 6. Standard Section Order*



*Documents shall maintain the following section order whenever applicable.*



*```text*

*1. Purpose*



*2. Objectives*



*3. Principles / Overview*



*4. Main Content*



*5. Compliance*



*References*



*Related Documents*



*Revision History*



*Approval*

*```*



*This sequence provides a predictable reading experience across the repository.*



*---*



*# 7. References Template*



*Every document shall contain a References section.*



*Example:*



*```markdown*

*# References*



*- GOV-0000 Engineering Standard*

*- GOV-0005 Coding Standards*

*```*



*If no references exist, the section shall contain:*



*```markdown*

*None.*

*```*



*---*



*# 8. Related Documents Template*



*Every document shall include navigation to adjacent documentation.*



*Example:*



*```markdown*

*# Related Documents*



*## Previous*



*- GOV-0007 Documentation Standards*



*## Next*



*- GOV-0009 Document Lifecycle*



*## Related*



*- GOV-INDEX Documentation Index*

*```*



*Broken references shall be corrected immediately.*



*---*



*# 9. Revision History Template*



*Every engineering document shall preserve its revision history.*



*Template:*



*```markdown*

*# Revision History*



*| Version | Date | Author | Description |*

*|----------|------|--------|-------------|*

*| 1.0.0 | YYYY-MM-DD | Francisco Javier Sánchez Mariscal | Initial version. |*

*```*



*Historical entries shall never be deleted.*



*---*



*# 10. Approval Template*



*Every document concludes with an Approval section.*



*Template:*



*```markdown*

*# Approval*



*This document becomes effective immediately upon approval by the Project Owner.*



*Future revisions shall follow Semantic Versioning principles and shall be documented in the Revision History section.*

*```*



*---*



*# 11. Specialized Templates*



*Different document domains may extend the standard template.*



*Examples include:*



*- Governance Documents*

*- Architecture Documents*

*- Product Requirements*

*- Design System Documents*

*- Case Studies*

*- Deployment Guides*

*- Runbooks*

*- ADRs*



*Specialized templates shall inherit the standard structure defined in this document.*



*---*



*# 12. Template Evolution*



*Documentation templates evolve together with the project.*



*Any structural modification affecting all documents shall be introduced by updating this document before modifying individual documents.*



*Templates are version-controlled engineering artifacts.*



*---*



*# 13. Compliance*



*Compliance with this standard is mandatory.*



*Documents that do not follow the approved template shall be considered incomplete until updated.*
