*# 1. Purpose*



*This document establishes the official Risk Management framework for the Francisco Sánchez Portfolio project.*



*The objective is to proactively identify, evaluate, prioritize, mitigate, monitor, and communicate risks that may impact the project's successful delivery, quality, maintainability, security, or long-term evolution.*



*Risk management is considered a continuous engineering activity rather than a one-time planning exercise.*



*---*



*# 2. Objectives*



*This document aims to:*



*- Identify project risks early.*

*- Reduce uncertainty.*

*- Improve engineering decisions.*

*- Protect project quality.*

*- Support business continuity.*

*- Encourage proactive planning.*

*- Establish a repeatable risk management process.*



*---*



*# 3. Risk Management Philosophy*



*Every engineering decision introduces some degree of risk.*



*Risks shall be identified before implementation whenever possible.*



*Ignoring a known risk is considered an engineering failure.*



*The project favors proactive mitigation over reactive correction.*



*---*



*# 4. Risk Management Process*



*All identified risks shall follow the lifecycle below.*



*```text*

*Identify*

&#x20;   *↓*

*Assess*

&#x20;   *↓*

*Prioritize*

&#x20;   *↓*

*Mitigate*

&#x20;   *↓*

*Monitor*

&#x20;   *↓*

*Close*

*```*



*The process is iterative and continuous throughout the project lifecycle.*



*---*



*# 5. Risk Categories*



*Risks shall be classified into one or more of the following categories:*



*- Technical*

*- Architectural*

*- Operational*

*- Security*

*- Performance*

*- Infrastructure*

*- Documentation*

*- Deployment*

*- Business*

*- Legal*

*- Third-Party Dependencies*

*- User Experience*

*- Accessibility*

*- Maintainability*



*Additional categories may be introduced as the project evolves.*



*---*



*# 6. Risk Assessment*



*Each risk shall be evaluated using two dimensions:*



*### Probability*



*- Low*

*- Medium*

*- High*



*### Impact*



*- Low*

*- Medium*

*- High*

*- Critical*



*The combination of Probability and Impact determines the overall risk priority.*



*---*



*# 7. Risk Priority Matrix*



*| Probability | Impact | Priority |*

*|-------------|--------|----------|*

*| Low | Low | Low |*

*| Low | Medium | Low |*

*| Medium | Medium | Medium |*

*| High | Medium | High |*

*| High | High | Critical |*

*| Medium | Critical | Critical |*



*This matrix serves as a guideline for prioritization.*



*---*



*# 8. Risk Register*



*All significant risks shall be recorded in the project Risk Register.*



*Each entry shall include:*



*- Risk ID*

*- Description*

*- Category*

*- Probability*

*- Impact*

*- Priority*

*- Mitigation Strategy*

*- Owner*

*- Status*

*- Review Date*



*The Risk Register shall remain under version control.*



*---*



*# 9. Risk Response Strategies*



*The following response strategies are supported:*



*### Avoid*



*Modify the project to eliminate the risk.*



*### Mitigate*



*Reduce either the probability or impact.*



*### Transfer*



*Shift responsibility to another party or service.*



*### Accept*



*Acknowledge the risk and monitor it without immediate action.*



*Every identified risk shall have a documented response strategy.*



*---*



*# 10. Risk Ownership*



*Every risk shall have an assigned owner responsible for:*



*- Monitoring.*

*- Mitigation.*

*- Communication.*

*- Status updates.*

*- Closure.*



*Unassigned risks are considered unmanaged risks.*



*---*



*# 11. Continuous Monitoring*



*Risk management is an ongoing activity.*



*Risks shall be reviewed:*



*- Before major releases.*

*- After architectural changes.*

*- Following production incidents.*

*- During project planning.*

*- After significant dependency updates.*



*Monitoring shall be documented whenever material changes occur.*



*---*



*# 12. Escalation*



*Risks classified as \*\*Critical\*\* shall be escalated immediately to the Project Owner.*



*Escalation may result in:*



*- Scope adjustments.*

*- Architecture reviews.*

*- Additional testing.*

*- Schedule changes.*

*- Implementation pauses.*



*Project quality shall always take precedence over delivery speed.*



*---*



*# 13. Documentation*



*Risk-related decisions shall be documented whenever they affect:*



*- Architecture.*

*- Infrastructure.*

*- Security.*

*- Deployment.*

*- Business continuity.*



*Major mitigation decisions should reference an Architecture Decision Record (ADR) whenever applicable.*



*---*



*# 14. Risk Review*



*Periodic reviews shall verify:*



*- Existing risks remain accurate.*

*- Closed risks remain closed.*

*- New risks are identified.*

*- Mitigation strategies remain effective.*



*Risk reviews should be performed alongside project planning activities.*



*---*



*# 15. Compliance*



*Compliance with this Risk Management framework is mandatory.*



*Engineering decisions that knowingly introduce unmanaged high-risk conditions shall require explicit approval by the Project Owner.*
