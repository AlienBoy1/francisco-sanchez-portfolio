*# 1. Purpose*



*This document defines the official lifecycle of engineering documentation within the Francisco Sánchez Portfolio project.*



*Every document progresses through a controlled sequence of states from creation to retirement. This lifecycle ensures documentation remains accurate, relevant, maintainable, and aligned with the current state of the project.*



*Documentation is considered a living engineering asset and shall evolve alongside the software it describes.*



*---*



*# 2. Objectives*



*This document aims to:*



*- Standardize document evolution.*

*- Improve documentation quality.*

*- Prevent obsolete documentation.*

*- Increase traceability.*

*- Support long-term maintainability.*

*- Establish review responsibilities.*

*- Preserve historical engineering knowledge.*



*---*



*# 3. Documentation Philosophy*



*Documentation shall evolve continuously.*



*Documents are never considered complete simply because they were written.*



*Instead, every document shall:*



*- Be reviewed.*

*- Be updated.*

*- Be versioned.*

*- Be maintained.*

*- Be archived when necessary.*



*Outdated documentation is considered technical debt.*



*---*



*# 4. Lifecycle States*



*Every engineering document shall progress through the following lifecycle.*



*```text*

*Draft*

&#x20;  *↓*

*Under Review*

&#x20;  *↓*

*Approved*

&#x20;  *↓*

*Published*

&#x20;  *↓*

*Maintained*

&#x20;  *↓*

*Deprecated (optional)*

&#x20;  *↓*

*Archived (optional)*

*```*



*Not every document will necessarily reach every state.*



*---*



*# 5. Draft*



*Status:*



*```text*

*Draft*

*```*



*Characteristics:*



*- Initial version.*

*- May contain incomplete sections.*

*- Subject to significant changes.*

*- Not considered authoritative.*



*Draft documents shall not be referenced as official engineering standards.*



*---*



*# 6. Under Review*



*Status:*



*```text*

*Under Review*

*```*



*Characteristics:*



*- Technical review in progress.*

*- Structure validated.*

*- Technical accuracy verified.*

*- Feedback incorporated.*



*Documents may return to Draft if major revisions are required.*



*---*



*# 7. Approved*



*Status:*



*```text*

*Approved*

*```*



*Characteristics:*



*- Technically complete.*

*- Governance compliant.*

*- Ready for publication.*

*- Considered the official project reference.*



*Only approved documents establish engineering standards.*



*---*



*# 8. Published*



*Status:*



*```text*

*Published*

*```*



*Published documentation becomes part of the project's official knowledge base.*



*Published documents shall be:*



*- Discoverable.*

*- Indexed.*

*- Linked.*

*- Version controlled.*



*---*



*# 9. Maintained*



*Documentation shall remain under continuous maintenance.*



*Maintenance activities include:*



*- Updating examples.*

*- Correcting inaccuracies.*

*- Reflecting architectural changes.*

*- Updating references.*

*- Synchronizing with implementation.*



*Maintenance preserves documentation quality throughout the project lifecycle.*



*---*



*# 10. Deprecated*



*Status:*



*```text*

*Deprecated*

*```*



*Documents become deprecated when:*



*- Replaced by newer standards.*

*- No longer applicable.*

*- Superseded by architectural evolution.*



*Deprecated documents shall remain accessible for historical reference.*



*---*



*# 11. Archived*



*Status:*



*```text*

*Archived*

*```*



*Archived documentation:*



*- Is read-only.*

*- Is no longer maintained.*

*- Remains available for traceability.*

*- Preserves historical project knowledge.*



*Archived documents shall never be deleted without explicit approval.*



*---*



*# 12. Version Updates*



*Documentation follows Semantic Versioning.*



*| Change Type | Version Increment |*

*|--------------|------------------|*

*| Editorial corrections | PATCH |*

*| New sections | MINOR |*

*| Breaking structural changes | MAJOR |*



*Every version update shall be recorded in the Revision History section.*



*---*



*# 13. Review Frequency*



*Engineering documentation shall be reviewed periodically.*



*Recommended cadence:*



*| Document Type | Review Frequency |*

*|---------------|-----------------|*

*| Governance | Every major release |*

*| Architecture | When architecture changes |*

*| Product | As requirements evolve |*

*| Design | When the design system changes |*

*| Deployment | After infrastructure updates |*



*Reviews should verify both technical accuracy and documentation quality.*



*---*



*# 14. Ownership*



*Every document shall have a clearly identified owner.*



*The owner is responsible for:*



*- Accuracy.*

*- Maintenance.*

*- Updates.*

*- Versioning.*

*- Review coordination.*



*Ownership may change over the project's lifetime.*



*---*



*# 15. Traceability*



*Documentation changes shall remain traceable.*



*Each revision shall include:*



*- Version.*

*- Date.*

*- Author.*

*- Summary of changes.*



*Historical information shall never be removed.*



*---*



*# 16. Compliance*



*Compliance with this lifecycle is mandatory.*



*Documentation that bypasses the defined lifecycle shall not be considered official project documentation.*
