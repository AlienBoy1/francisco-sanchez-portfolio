*# 1. Purpose*



*This document defines the core design principles that guide every visual, interactive, and user experience decision throughout the Francisco Sánchez Portfolio.*



*These principles establish the philosophical foundation of the Design System and serve as the primary criteria for evaluating new interfaces, components, and interaction patterns.*



*Every design decision shall be consistent with these principles.*



*---*



*# 2. Design Philosophy*



*The portfolio is designed to communicate engineering excellence.*



*Its purpose is not to impress through visual excess, but to demonstrate clarity, professionalism, technical maturity, and attention to detail.*



*Design exists to strengthen communication, never to compete with content.*



*---*



*# 3. Core Principles*



*The Design System is governed by the following principles.*



*---*



*## Principle 1 — Purpose Before Decoration*



*Every visual element shall serve a functional purpose.*



*Decorative elements shall only exist when they reinforce comprehension, hierarchy, or brand identity.*



*Visual noise shall be eliminated.*



*---*



*## Principle 2 — Clarity Over Complexity*



*Interfaces shall prioritize understanding.*



*Users must immediately recognize:*



*- where they are,*

*- what they can do,*

*- and what information is most important.*



*Complex interactions shall appear simple.*



*---*



*## Principle 3 — Consistency Creates Trust*



*Consistent interfaces reduce cognitive load.*



*The same interaction shall always produce the same result.*



*Components shall behave predictably across the entire application.*



*---*



*## Principle 4 — Content Is the Primary Interface*



*The portfolio exists to communicate professional experience and engineering capability.*



*Content shall always take precedence over decoration.*



*Typography, spacing, and layout shall reinforce readability.*



*---*



*## Principle 5 — Design for Performance*



*Performance is part of the user experience.*



*Every visual decision shall consider:*



*- rendering cost,*

*- bundle size,*

*- animation efficiency,*

*- loading behavior,*

*- responsiveness.*



*Beautiful interfaces should also be fast.*



*---*



*## Principle 6 — Accessibility by Default*



*Accessibility is not an enhancement.*



*It is a fundamental engineering requirement.*



*Every interface shall be designed to support users with diverse abilities without requiring alternative implementations.*



*---*



*## Principle 7 — Progressive Disclosure*



*Complexity shall be revealed gradually.*



*Users should initially see only the information necessary for their current task.*



*Additional information shall appear naturally as interaction progresses.*



*---*



*## Principle 8 — Component First*



*Every interface shall be assembled from reusable components.*



*Pages shall compose components instead of introducing one-off solutions.*



*Reusable patterns reduce maintenance cost and improve consistency.*



*---*



*## Principle 9 — Systems Over Pages*



*The Design System shall define reusable rules rather than isolated page layouts.*



*Interfaces shall emerge from the system instead of being individually handcrafted.*



*The system is the product.*



*---*



*## Principle 10 — Long-Term Maintainability*



*Design decisions shall favor sustainability.*



*Short-term visual trends shall never compromise long-term maintainability.*



*The Design System is expected to evolve over many years.*



*---*



*# 4. Decision Framework*



*Every new design proposal shall answer the following questions before approval.*



*- Does it solve a real user problem?*

*- Is it consistent with existing patterns?*

*- Can it be reused?*

*- Is it accessible?*

*- Does it improve clarity?*

*- Does it reduce complexity?*

*- Can it be implemented efficiently?*

*- Does it align with the Brand Foundation?*

*- Will it remain valuable in the future?*



*If any answer is negative, the proposal shall be reviewed before implementation.*



*---*



*# 5. Relationship with Brand Identity*



*The Design Principles translate the Brand Identity into visual and interactive behaviors.*



*The Brand defines:*



*- who we are,*

*- how we communicate,*

*- and what we value.*



*The Design Principles define how those values appear within the interface.*



*---*



*# 6. Relationship with Engineering*



*Every Design Principle shall be implementable through software.*



*Visual rules that cannot be consistently implemented shall not become part of the Design System.*



*Design and engineering shall evolve together.*



*---*



*# 7. Governance*



*Design Principles are considered foundational artifacts.*



*They are expected to remain stable over the lifetime of the project.*



*Any modification requires architectural justification and formal review.*



*---*



*# 8. Compliance*



*Every page, layout, component, animation, and interaction shall comply with these Design Principles.*



*Exceptions require approval through the project's governance process.*
