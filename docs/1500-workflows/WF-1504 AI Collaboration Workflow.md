*# 1. Purpose*



*This document defines the official AI Collaboration Workflow for the Francisco Sánchez Portfolio AI-First engineering ecosystem.*



*The workflow establishes the standardized process governing collaboration between human engineers and Artificial Intelligence systems during engineering activities.*



*The objective is to maximize engineering productivity while preserving human authority, architectural integrity, governance compliance, and software quality.*



*Every AI-assisted engineering activity shall follow this workflow.*



*---*



*# 2. Objectives*



*The AI Collaboration Workflow aims to:*



*- Standardize Human-AI collaboration.*

*- Improve engineering productivity.*

*- Preserve engineering quality.*

*- Ensure architectural consistency.*

*- Reduce repetitive work.*

*- Maintain governance.*

*- Improve knowledge reuse.*

*- Enable scalable AI-assisted development.*



*---*



*# 3. Workflow Philosophy*



*Artificial Intelligence is an engineering collaborator.*



*AI does not replace engineers.*



*AI augments engineering capabilities.*



*Human engineers retain ownership of:*



*- Decisions.*

*- Architecture.*

*- Governance.*

*- Security.*

*- Production approval.*



*AI provides assistance—not authority.*



*---*



*# 4. Scope*



*This workflow governs every interaction between engineers and AI systems including:*



*- Software development.*

*- Documentation.*

*- Architecture.*

*- Planning.*

*- Code review.*

*- Refactoring.*

*- Testing.*

*- Debugging.*

*- Prompt generation.*

*- Engineering analysis.*



*It applies regardless of the AI platform being used.*



*---*



*# 5. Participants*



*Typical participants include:*



*- Project Owner*

*- Software Engineer*

*- Cursor*

*- ChatGPT*

*- Claude*

*- Gemini*

*- GitHub Copilot*

*- Internal AI Agents*

*- Future AI Systems*



*Every participant shall operate within documented responsibilities.*

*# 19. Decision Gates*



*Workflow progression requires:*



*- Context Complete.*

*- Task Clearly Defined.*

*- AI Output Generated.*

*- Human Validation Completed.*

*- Architecture Verified.*

*- Documentation Updated.*

*- Approval Granted.*



*Failure at any Decision Gate pauses the workflow.*

*All Quality Gates are mandatory.*

*# 24. AI Operational Contract*



*## Responsibilities*



*Every AI participant shall:*



*- Respect repository governance.*

*- Follow engineering workflows.*

*- Preserve architecture.*

*- Produce explainable outputs.*

*- Report limitations.*

*- Avoid unsupported assumptions.*



*---*



*## Authorized Actions*



*AI may:*



*- Generate artifacts.*

*- Analyze documentation.*

*- Explain architecture.*

*- Suggest improvements.*

*- Produce engineering reports.*

*- Assist engineering workflows.*



*---*



*## Restricted Actions*



*AI shall not:*



*- Approve production changes.*

*- Override human decisions.*

*- Ignore repository standards.*

*- Modify governance autonomously.*

*- Skip workflow stages.*

*- Deploy systems without authorization.*



*---*



*## Success Criteria*



*The AI Collaboration Workflow is successful when:*



*- Human productivity improves.*

*- Engineering quality increases.*

*- Documentation remains synchronized.*

*- Architecture remains consistent.*

*- Governance compliance reaches 100%.*

*- AI becomes a reliable engineering collaborator.*



*---*



*## Failure Conditions*



*The workflow fails when:*



*- AI operates without context.*

*- Human validation is skipped.*

*- Documentation becomes inconsistent.*

*- Governance is bypassed.*

*- Architectural integrity is compromised.*

*- AI assumptions replace engineering evidence.*



*---*



*## Escalation*



*Any AI-generated artifact affecting production systems, architecture, governance, security, infrastructure, or organizational standards shall require explicit approval from the Project Owner before integration.*



*---*



*# 25. Governance*



*The AI Collaboration Workflow governs every Human-AI engineering interaction within the AI-First engineering ecosystem.*



*Every AI platform, agent, IDE integration, orchestration engine, and future autonomous engineering assistant shall comply with the principles established herein.*
