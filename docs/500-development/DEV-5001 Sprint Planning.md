*# 1. Purpose*



*This document defines the Sprint Planning process for the Francisco Sánchez Portfolio.*



*It establishes how development work shall be planned, prioritized, estimated, executed, and reviewed throughout the project lifecycle.*



*The objective is to provide a predictable and sustainable engineering workflow while maintaining the highest standards of software quality.*



*---*



*# 2. Objectives*



*The Sprint Planning process aims to:*



*- Organize development into manageable iterations.*

*- Prioritize high-value work.*

*- Reduce planning uncertainty.*

*- Maintain alignment with product and architecture goals.*

*- Enable continuous progress.*

*- Preserve engineering quality.*

*- Support transparent project tracking.*



*---*



*# 3. Planning Philosophy*



*Sprint Planning is an engineering planning activity rather than a scheduling exercise.*



*The goal of each sprint is to deliver stable, documented, and reviewable increments of the project.*



*Incomplete or unverified work shall not be considered completed.*



*---*



*# 4. Guiding Principles*



*Sprint Planning follows these principles.*



*- Documentation First.*

*- Architecture Before Implementation.*

*- Small, Incremental Deliverables.*

*- Sustainable Development Pace.*

*- Continuous Validation.*

*- Quality Over Velocity.*

*- Explicit Prioritization.*

*- Predictable Execution.*



*Every sprint shall reflect these principles.*



*---*



*# 5. Sprint Structure*



*Each sprint shall include the following stages.*



*1. Sprint Preparation.*

*2. Sprint Planning.*

*3. Implementation.*

*4. Continuous Review.*

*5. Validation.*

*6. Sprint Review.*

*7. Sprint Retrospective.*



*Each stage shall produce documented outcomes.*



*---*



*# 6. Sprint Inputs*



*Sprint Planning shall consider the following inputs.*



*- Product Roadmap.*

*- Functional Requirements.*

*- Architecture Documentation.*

*- Design System.*

*- Development Roadmap.*

*- Risk Register.*

*- Technical Debt Register.*

*- Outstanding defects.*

*- Previous sprint results.*



*Planning decisions shall be based on current project documentation.*



*---*



*# 7. Sprint Outputs*



*Every sprint shall produce:*



*- Completed development tasks.*

*- Updated documentation.*

*- Reviewed source code.*

*- Verified functionality.*

*- Updated backlog.*

*- Identified risks.*

*- Lessons learned.*



*Deliverables shall be measurable and reviewable.*



*---*



*# 8. Work Prioritization*



*Development work shall be prioritized according to:*



*1. Architectural dependencies.*

*2. Product value.*

*3. Technical risk.*

*4. Implementation complexity.*

*5. Long-term maintainability.*



*Priority shall never be determined solely by implementation convenience.*



*---*



*# 9. Task Granularity*



*Tasks shall be sufficiently small to enable accurate estimation and continuous validation.*



*Large features shall be decomposed into smaller engineering tasks with clearly defined responsibilities and acceptance criteria.*



*---*



*# 10. Definition of Ready*



*A work item is considered ready for implementation when:*



*- Requirements are documented.*

*- Dependencies are identified.*

*- Design decisions are available.*

*- Architectural impact is understood.*

*- Acceptance criteria are defined.*

*- Required documentation exists.*



*Items failing these conditions shall not enter a sprint.*



*---*



*# 11. Definition of Done*



*A work item is considered complete only when:*



*- Implementation is finished.*

*- Code has been reviewed.*

*- Tests pass successfully.*

*- Documentation has been updated.*

*- Architectural compliance is verified.*

*- No known critical defects remain.*

*- Acceptance criteria are fully satisfied.*



*Completion is determined by quality, not by elapsed time.*



*---*



*# 12. Sprint Review*



*At the conclusion of each sprint, completed work shall be reviewed to verify:*



*- Planned objectives were achieved.*

*- Engineering standards were followed.*

*- Documentation remains synchronized.*

*- Architectural integrity is preserved.*

*- Outstanding issues are identified.*



*Only approved work shall be considered complete.*



*---*



*# 13. Sprint Retrospective*



*Each sprint shall conclude with a retrospective.*



*The retrospective shall identify:*



*- Successful practices.*

*- Process improvements.*

*- Technical challenges.*

*- Planning inaccuracies.*

*- Opportunities for future optimization.*



*Lessons learned shall inform subsequent sprints.*



*---*



*# 14. Governance*



*Sprint Planning documentation shall remain synchronized with actual project execution.*



*Changes to planning practices require:*



*- Documented rationale.*

*- Impact assessment.*

*- Approval by the Project Owner.*



*Significant process changes may require an Architecture Decision Record (ADR).*



*---*



*# 15. Compliance*



*All sprint activities shall comply with:*



*- Portfolio Engineering Standard.*

*- Repository Governance.*

*- Product Documentation.*

*- Design System.*

*- Architecture Documentation.*

*- Development Roadmap.*



*Exceptions require documented approval.*
