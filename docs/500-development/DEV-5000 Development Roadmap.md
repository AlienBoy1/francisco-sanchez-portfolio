*# 1. Purpose*



*This document defines the Development Roadmap for the Francisco Sánchez Portfolio.*



*It establishes the engineering execution plan, major development phases, milestones, dependencies, and governance required to guide the project from initial planning to its first production release and future evolution.*



*The roadmap serves as the authoritative planning reference for development activities.*



*---*



*# 2. Objectives*



*The Development Roadmap aims to:*



*- Organize development into manageable phases.*

*- Establish a logical implementation sequence.*

*- Define major engineering milestones.*

*- Improve planning predictability.*

*- Reduce project risk.*

*- Support incremental delivery.*

*- Maintain alignment with the project's architectural vision.*



*---*



*# 3. Development Philosophy*



*Development shall follow a documentation-first approach.*



*Each implementation activity shall be preceded by the necessary architectural, design, and product documentation.*



*Every phase shall produce stable, reviewable, and maintainable deliverables before progressing to the next.*



*Progress shall prioritize quality over speed.*



*---*



*# 4. Guiding Principles*



*The roadmap follows these principles.*



*- Documentation First.*

*- Architecture Before Implementation.*

*- Incremental Delivery.*

*- Quality Over Velocity.*

*- Continuous Validation.*

*- Sustainable Development.*

*- Controlled Scope Evolution.*

*- Long-Term Maintainability.*



*These principles govern every development activity.*



*---*



*# 5. Development Phases*



*The project is organized into the following high-level phases.*



*| Phase | Description |*

*|--------|-------------|*

*| Foundation | Governance, branding, product definition, design system, and architecture. |*

*| Core Implementation | Development of the application foundation and essential features. |*

*| Feature Expansion | Incremental implementation of remaining portfolio capabilities. |*

*| Quality Assurance | Validation, optimization, accessibility, testing, and security hardening. |*

*| Production Release | Final deployment, monitoring, documentation review, and public launch. |*

*| Continuous Evolution | Ongoing maintenance, enhancements, and future releases. |*



*---*



*# 6. Milestones*



*Major milestones include:*



*- Governance completed.*

*- Brand identity completed.*

*- Product documentation completed.*

*- Design System completed.*

*- Architecture completed.*

*- Development foundation completed.*

*- MVP feature complete.*

*- Quality verification complete.*

*- Production deployment complete.*

*- Version 1.0 officially released.*



*Milestones shall represent measurable engineering achievements.*



*---*



*# 7. Dependencies*



*Development activities shall respect established dependencies.*



*Examples include:*



*- Product documentation precedes implementation.*

*- Design System precedes UI development.*

*- Architecture precedes software implementation.*

*- Security and performance considerations are incorporated throughout development.*

*- Deployment preparation follows successful validation.*



*Dependencies shall not be bypassed without documented justification.*



*---*



*# 8. Incremental Delivery*



*Development shall progress through incremental iterations.*



*Each iteration shall produce:*



*- Stable deliverables.*

*- Updated documentation.*

*- Verified functionality.*

*- Architectural consistency.*

*- Reviewable outcomes.*



*Incomplete work shall not compromise project stability.*



*---*



*# 9. Progress Tracking*



*Project progress shall be evaluated through measurable indicators.*



*Representative metrics include:*



*- Documentation completion.*

*- Feature completion.*

*- Milestone completion.*

*- Test coverage.*

*- Architectural compliance.*

*- Technical debt.*

*- Defect resolution.*



*Progress shall reflect delivered quality rather than the volume of implemented code.*



*---*



*# 10. Risk Management*



*Development risks shall be continuously identified and monitored.*



*Representative risks include:*



*- Scope expansion.*

*- Technical uncertainty.*

*- Documentation drift.*

*- Architectural deviations.*

*- Dependency changes.*

*- Performance regressions.*



*Risks shall be addressed proactively through planning and governance.*



*---*



*# 11. Review Process*



*Each development phase shall conclude with a formal review.*



*Reviews shall verify:*



*- Completion of planned objectives.*

*- Compliance with engineering standards.*

*- Architectural consistency.*

*- Documentation updates.*

*- Readiness for the next phase.*



*No phase shall advance without successful review and approval.*



*---*



*# 12. Governance*



*The Development Roadmap shall remain synchronized with the actual project state.*



*Roadmap modifications require:*



*- Documented rationale.*

*- Impact assessment.*

*- Revision history update.*

*- Approval by the Project Owner.*



*Significant changes may require an Architecture Decision Record (ADR).*



*---*



*# 13. Compliance*



*All development activities shall comply with:*



*- Portfolio Engineering Standard.*

*- Repository governance.*

*- Product documentation.*

*- Design System.*

*- Architecture documentation.*

*- Development governance.*



*Exceptions require documented approval.*
