*# 1. Purpose*



*This document defines the Risk Register for the Francisco Sánchez Portfolio.*



*The Risk Register serves as the official repository for identifying, assessing, monitoring, and mitigating risks that may affect the successful delivery, quality, security, or maintainability of the project.*



*It provides a structured and continuously updated view of project risks throughout the development lifecycle.*



*---*



*# 2. Objectives*



*The Risk Register aims to:*



*- Identify project risks proactively.*

*- Assess probability and impact consistently.*

*- Define mitigation strategies.*

*- Assign ownership for each risk.*

*- Monitor risk evolution over time.*

*- Support informed decision-making.*

*- Improve project resilience.*



*---*



*# 3. Risk Management Philosophy*



*Risk management is a continuous engineering activity.*



*Risks shall be identified as early as possible, evaluated objectively, monitored throughout the project, and addressed before they materialize whenever feasible.*



*The absence of documented risks shall never be interpreted as the absence of risk.*



*---*



*# 4. Guiding Principles*



*Risk management follows these principles.*



*- Early Identification.*

*- Objective Assessment.*

*- Continuous Monitoring.*

*- Transparent Communication.*

*- Accountability.*

*- Preventive Action.*

*- Evidence-Based Decisions.*

*- Continuous Improvement.*



*---*



*# 5. Risk Classification*



*Risks shall be classified into one or more of the following categories.*



*| Category | Description |*

*|----------|-------------|*

*| Technical | Architecture, implementation, dependencies, scalability. |*

*| Product | Requirements, scope, usability, stakeholder expectations. |*

*| Security | Vulnerabilities, data exposure, authentication, authorization. |*

*| Performance | Resource consumption, responsiveness, optimization. |*

*| Quality | Defects, testing, documentation drift. |*

*| Schedule | Delays, planning deviations, milestone slippage. |*

*| Operational | Infrastructure, deployment, tooling, third-party services. |*

*| Organizational | Resource availability, communication, governance. |*



*Additional categories may be introduced when justified.*



*---*



*# 6. Risk Attributes*



*Every recorded risk shall include, at minimum:*



*- Risk Identifier.*

*- Title.*

*- Description.*

*- Category.*

*- Probability.*

*- Impact.*

*- Overall Risk Level.*

*- Owner.*

*- Mitigation Strategy.*

*- Contingency Plan.*

*- Current Status.*

*- Date Identified.*

*- Last Review Date.*



*These attributes ensure consistent evaluation and traceability.*



*---*



*# 7. Risk Assessment*



*Risks shall be evaluated using a qualitative risk matrix.*



*Probability levels:*



*- Low*

*- Medium*

*- High*



*Impact levels:*



*- Low*

*- Medium*

*- High*



*The combination of probability and impact determines the overall risk level.*



*Assessment criteria shall remain consistent across the project.*



*---*



*# 8. Risk Lifecycle*



*Each risk progresses through the following lifecycle.*



*1. Identified.*

*2. Assessed.*

*3. Assigned.*

*4. Mitigated.*

*5. Monitored.*

*6. Closed.*



*Risk status shall always reflect the current project reality.*



*---*



*# 9. Mitigation Strategies*



*Every significant risk shall include one or more mitigation strategies.*



*Representative approaches include:*



*- Prevention.*

*- Reduction.*

*- Transfer.*

*- Acceptance.*

*- Continuous Monitoring.*



*Mitigation actions shall be proportional to the assessed risk level.*



*---*



*# 10. Risk Reviews*



*The Risk Register shall be reviewed regularly.*



*Reviews shall verify:*



*- New risks.*

*- Changes in probability.*

*- Changes in impact.*

*- Effectiveness of mitigation actions.*

*- Closed risks.*

*- Emerging risks.*



*Risk reviews shall occur at least once per development iteration.*



*---*



*# 11. Reporting*



*Risk status shall be communicated through project governance.*



*Reports shall include:*



*- High-priority risks.*

*- Recently identified risks.*

*- Risks requiring escalation.*

*- Mitigation progress.*

*- Closed risks.*



*Risk reporting supports informed project decisions.*



*---*



*# 12. Governance*



*The Risk Register is a controlled engineering document.*



*Changes require:*



*- Updated assessment.*

*- Supporting rationale.*

*- Revision tracking.*

*- Approval when required.*



*Risk ownership shall remain clearly assigned.*



*---*



*# 13. Compliance*



*All project risks with Medium or High impact shall be documented in this register.*



*Critical risks shall be reviewed before any production release.*



*Exceptions require documented approval.*
