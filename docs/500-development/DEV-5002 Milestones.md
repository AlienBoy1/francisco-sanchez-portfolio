*# 1. Purpose*



*This document defines the official project milestones for the Francisco Sánchez Portfolio.*



*Milestones represent significant engineering achievements that demonstrate measurable progress toward the successful completion of the project.*



*Each milestone serves as a formal validation checkpoint before advancing to subsequent phases of development.*



*---*



*# 2. Objectives*



*The Milestones process aims to:*



*- Establish measurable project checkpoints.*

*- Monitor engineering progress.*

*- Verify completion of major deliverables.*

*- Reduce project risk.*

*- Support controlled development.*

*- Improve project visibility.*

*- Ensure readiness before advancing to the next phase.*



*---*



*# 3. Milestone Philosophy*



*A milestone represents the successful completion of a meaningful engineering objective.*



*Milestones are based on verified outcomes rather than estimated effort or elapsed time.*



*Progress shall be measured by completed deliverables that satisfy predefined acceptance criteria.*



*---*



*# 4. Guiding Principles*



*Milestone management follows these principles.*



*- Objective Validation.*

*- Documentation First.*

*- Incremental Progress.*

*- Quality Over Speed.*

*- Engineering Excellence.*

*- Traceability.*

*- Repeatability.*

*- Continuous Improvement.*



*Every milestone shall reflect these principles.*



*---*



*# 5. Milestone Hierarchy*



*Project milestones are organized into strategic levels.*



*| Level | Description |*

*|--------|-------------|*

*| Foundation | Core governance and planning completed. |*

*| Architecture | Product, design, and architecture fully defined. |*

*| Implementation | Core application successfully developed. |*

*| Validation | Quality verification completed. |*

*| Release | Production deployment completed. |*

*| Evolution | Continuous improvement after Version 1.0. |*



*Each level builds upon the successful completion of the previous one.*

*---*



*# 7. Acceptance Criteria*



*A milestone shall only be considered complete when:*



*- Planned deliverables are finished.*

*- Documentation is approved.*

*- Engineering standards are satisfied.*

*- Architectural compliance is verified.*

*- Outstanding critical issues have been resolved.*

*- Formal approval has been granted by the Project Owner.*



*Partial completion shall not qualify as milestone completion.*



*---*



*# 8. Progress Evaluation*



*Milestone progress shall be evaluated using objective indicators.*



*Representative indicators include:*



*- Document approval.*

*- Feature completion.*

*- Test completion.*

*- Architectural validation.*

*- Compliance verification.*

*- Technical debt assessment.*

*- Risk status.*



*Progress shall be evidence-based.*



*---*



*# 9. Dependencies*



*Milestones shall respect project dependencies.*



*Examples include:*



*- Architecture milestones require completed Product and Design documentation.*

*- Implementation milestones require approved Architecture.*

*- Validation milestones require completed implementation.*

*- Production Release requires successful validation.*



*Dependencies shall not be bypassed without documented approval.*



*---*



*# 10. Review Process*



*Each milestone shall undergo a formal review.*



*The review shall verify:*



*- Completion of acceptance criteria.*

*- Documentation consistency.*

*- Architectural integrity.*

*- Quality compliance.*

*- Readiness for the next milestone.*



*Only approved milestones may be considered completed.*



*---*



*# 11. Change Management*



*Milestones may evolve during the project lifecycle.*



*Changes require:*



*- Documented rationale.*

*- Impact assessment.*

*- Revision History update.*

*- Approval by the Project Owner.*



*Major milestone modifications may require an Architecture Decision Record (ADR).*



*---*



*# 12. Governance*



*Milestones constitute official governance checkpoints.*



*Project planning, reporting, and release decisions shall reference milestone status.*



*Milestone records shall remain synchronized with actual project progress.*



*---*



*# 13. Compliance*



*All milestone activities shall comply with:*



*- Portfolio Engineering Standard.*

*- Repository Governance.*

*- Product Documentation.*

*- Design System.*

*- Architecture Documentation.*

*- Development Roadmap.*



*Exceptions require documented approval.*
