*# 1. Purpose*



*This document defines the official registry of current, planned, and future AI Agents within the Francisco Sánchez Portfolio engineering ecosystem.*



*The registry serves as the single authoritative catalog governing the long-term evolution of the AI Agent ecosystem.*



*Every AI Agent shall be registered before entering the engineering lifecycle defined in AGT-1202.*



*No operational AI Agent may exist outside this registry.*



*---*



*# 2. Objectives*



*The Future Agent Registry aims to:*



*- Maintain a centralized inventory of AI Agents.*

*- Prevent uncontrolled ecosystem growth.*

*- Support long-term planning.*

*- Preserve architectural consistency.*

*- Enable scalable specialization.*

*- Improve governance.*

*- Facilitate future orchestration.*

*- Provide complete ecosystem visibility.*



*---*



*# 3. Registry Philosophy*



*The AI ecosystem shall evolve intentionally.*



*New AI Agents shall originate from engineering needs rather than technological curiosity.*



*Every planned AI Agent represents a future engineering capability.*



*The registry is a strategic roadmap rather than an implementation backlog.*



*---*



*# 4. Registry Principles*



*Every registered AI Agent shall satisfy the following principles.*



*- Unique purpose.*

*- Explicit responsibilities.*

*- Architectural alignment.*

*- Governance compliance.*

*- Version traceability.*

*- Lifecycle management.*

*- Engineering value.*

*- Scalability.*



*---*



*# 5. Registry Structure*



*Every registry entry shall define:*



*- Agent Identifier.*

*- Agent Name.*

*- Domain.*

*- Purpose.*

*- Primary Responsibilities.*

*- Dependencies.*

*- Lifecycle Status.*

*- Priority.*

*- Planned Version.*

*- Notes.*



*---*



*# 6. Lifecycle Status*

*## Proposed*

*---*

*Approved for future implementation.*

*---*



*## Operational*



*Available for engineering workflows.*



*---*



*## Deprecated*



*Scheduled for retirement.*



*---*



*## Retired*

*# 12. Future Expansion*



*Future AI Agents may be introduced only when:*



*- An engineering need exists.*

*- Responsibilities are unique.*

*- Architectural compatibility is verified.*

*- Governance approval is obtained.*

*- Registry documentation is updated.*



*Every future AI Agent shall be added to this registry before implementation.*



*---*



*# 13. Registry Governance*



*The registry shall be reviewed periodically.*



*Reviews shall verify:*



*- Registry accuracy.*

*- Agent relevance.*

*- Duplicate responsibilities.*

*- Architectural consistency.*

*- Lifecycle status.*



*Registry updates shall remain version controlled.*



*---*



*# 14. Registry Traceability*



*Every registry modification shall record:*



*- Agent Identifier.*

*- Change Description.*

*- Responsible Authority.*

*- Date.*

*- Version.*

*- Related Documentation.*



*Historical registry entries shall never be deleted.*



*---*



*# 15. AI Operational Contract*



*## Responsibilities*



*Every AI Agent shall:*



*- Exist within the official registry.*

*- Maintain unique responsibilities.*

*- Respect lifecycle status.*

*- Preserve architectural consistency.*

*- Support ecosystem evolution.*



*---*



*## Authorized Actions*



*Agents may:*



*- Request registry inclusion.*

*- Recommend registry updates.*

*- Propose new specializations.*

*- Recommend retirement.*

*- Suggest responsibility refinements.*



*---*



*## Restricted Actions*



*Agents shall not:*



*- Self-register.*

*- Operate without registry approval.*

*- Duplicate existing responsibilities.*

*- Modify registry entries autonomously.*

*- Bypass governance.*



*---*



*## Success Criteria*



*The Future Agent Registry is successful when:*



*- Every operational AI Agent is registered.*

*- Responsibilities remain unique.*

*- Ecosystem growth remains controlled.*

*- Architectural consistency is preserved.*

*- Long-term planning becomes predictable.*



*---*



*## Failure Conditions*



*The registry fails when:*



*- Unregistered AI Agents exist.*

*- Responsibilities overlap excessively.*

*- Registry information becomes outdated.*

*- Governance is bypassed.*

*- Ecosystem evolution becomes uncontrolled.*



*---*



*## Escalation*



*Registry modifications affecting architecture, governance, or ecosystem evolution shall be approved by the Project Owner before implementation.*



*---*



*# 16. Governance*



*The Future Agent Registry is the authoritative source governing the composition and long-term evolution of the AI Agent ecosystem.*



*Every future AI Agent shall originate from this registry before entering the lifecycle defined in AGT-1202.*
