*# 1. Purpose*



*This document establishes the architectural foundation for the AI Agent ecosystem used throughout the Francisco Sánchez Portfolio.*



*It defines what an AI Agent is, its role within the engineering ecosystem, its relationship with AI Governance and Cursor Rules, and the principles governing collaboration between specialized agents.*



*The objective is to create a modular, scalable, deterministic, and governable multi-agent engineering platform.*



*---*



*# 2. Objectives*



*The AI Agent ecosystem aims to:*



*- Modularize engineering responsibilities.*

*- Reduce cognitive overload.*

*- Increase engineering quality.*

*- Improve specialization.*

*- Enable parallel engineering execution.*

*- Preserve governance consistency.*

*- Improve scalability.*

*- Support long-term ecosystem evolution.*



*---*



*# 3. AI Agent Philosophy*



*Artificial Intelligence shall not operate as a single general-purpose assistant.*



*Instead, engineering responsibilities shall be distributed across specialized AI Agents.*



*Each agent shall possess:*



*- A clearly defined purpose.*

*- Limited responsibilities.*

*- Explicit authority.*

*- Standardized behavior.*

*- Governed execution.*



*No agent shall attempt to solve every engineering problem.*



*Specialization is preferred over generalization.*



*---*



*# 4. Definition of an AI Agent*



*An AI Agent is an autonomous engineering component responsible for executing a specific category of engineering activities under the governance defined by this repository.*



*Every AI Agent shall:*



*- Operate independently.*

*- Follow AI Governance.*

*- Respect Cursor Rules.*

*- Collaborate with other agents.*

*- Produce deterministic engineering outcomes.*



*Agents are engineering specialists rather than conversational assistants.*



*---*



*# 5. Agent Design Principles*



*Every AI Agent shall satisfy the following principles.*



*## Single Responsibility*



*Each agent owns one engineering domain.*



*---*



*## Modularity*



*Agents remain independent.*



*---*



*## Determinism*



*Equivalent inputs shall produce equivalent engineering behavior.*



*---*



*## Traceability*



*Agent decisions shall remain explainable.*



*---*



*## Governance Compliance*



*Agents shall never violate approved engineering standards.*



*---*



*## Collaboration*



*Agents cooperate instead of competing.*



*---*



*## Scalability*



*New agents shall integrate without modifying existing agents.*



*---*

*# 8. Agent Responsibilities*



*Every agent shall define:*



*- Mission.*

*- Scope.*

*- Inputs.*

*- Outputs.*

*- Dependencies.*

*- Constraints.*

*- Authority.*

*- Success Criteria.*



*Responsibilities shall never overlap unnecessarily.*



*---*



*# 9. Agent Boundaries*



*Agents shall not:*



*- Perform unrelated responsibilities.*

*- Override governance.*

*- Modify another agent's responsibilities.*

*- Ignore engineering standards.*

*- Execute outside their authorized scope.*



*Boundaries preserve modularity.*



*---*



*# 10. Agent Lifecycle*



*Every AI Agent progresses through the following lifecycle.*



*1. Definition*

*2. Design*

*3. Approval*

*4. Registration*

*5. Deployment*

*6. Execution*

*7. Evaluation*

*8. Improvement*

*9. Retirement*



*No agent shall bypass lifecycle stages.*



*---*



*# 11. Agent Communication*



*Agents shall collaborate through structured communication.*



*Communication shall include:*



*- Engineering objective.*

*- Required inputs.*

*- Expected outputs.*

*- Context.*

*- Constraints.*

*- Validation requirements.*



*Agents shall exchange engineering knowledge rather than assumptions.*



*---*



*# 12. Agent Governance*



*Every AI Agent inherits:*



*- Project Governance.*

*- AI Governance.*

*- Cursor Rules.*



*Specialized behavior may extend these standards but shall never contradict them.*



*---*



*# 13. AI Operational Contract*



*## Responsibilities*



*Every AI Agent shall:*



*- Execute within its domain.*

*- Respect governance.*

*- Collaborate with other agents.*

*- Preserve engineering quality.*

*- Produce traceable decisions.*



*---*



*## Authorized Actions*



*Agents may:*



*- Execute specialized tasks.*

*- Request additional context.*

*- Delegate work.*

*- Recommend improvements.*

*- Escalate unresolved issues.*



*---*



*## Restricted Actions*



*Agents shall not:*



*- Override governance.*

*- Operate outside scope.*

*- Ignore Cursor Rules.*

*- Modify unrelated engineering artifacts.*

*- Make undocumented architectural decisions.*



*---*



*## Success Criteria*



*An AI Agent is successful when:*



*- Responsibilities are fulfilled.*

*- Scope is respected.*

*- Deliverables satisfy quality standards.*

*- Collaboration remains effective.*

*- Governance compliance is preserved.*



*---*



*## Failure Conditions*



*An AI Agent fails when:*



*- Responsibilities overlap improperly.*

*- Governance is violated.*

*- Engineering quality decreases.*

*- Collaboration fails.*

*- Deliverables become inconsistent.*



*---*



*## Escalation*



*Strategic conflicts between agents shall be escalated to the Project Owner before engineering execution continues.*



*---*



*# 14. Governance*



*The AI Agent Framework governs every present and future AI Agent within the engineering ecosystem.*



*All specialized agents shall inherit the principles defined herein unless superseded by a newer approved standard.*
