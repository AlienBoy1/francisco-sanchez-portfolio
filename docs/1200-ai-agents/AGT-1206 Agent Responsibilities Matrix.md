*# 1. Purpose*



*This document establishes the official responsibility allocation model governing every AI Agent operating within the Francisco Sánchez Portfolio engineering ecosystem.*



*The objective is to ensure that every engineering responsibility has a clearly defined owner while preventing duplication, ambiguity, responsibility conflicts, and uncontrolled execution.*



*Every engineering activity shall have one primary responsible AI Agent.*



*---*



*# 2. Objectives*



*The Agent Responsibilities Matrix aims to:*



*- Eliminate responsibility overlap.*

*- Improve engineering specialization.*

*- Standardize task ownership.*

*- Support scalable orchestration.*

*- Preserve governance.*

*- Improve collaboration.*

*- Enable deterministic execution.*

*- Facilitate future ecosystem growth.*



*---*



*# 3. Responsibility Philosophy*



*Responsibility shall be explicit.*



*Every engineering activity shall have:*



*- One owner.*

*- Defined authority.*

*- Clear boundaries.*

*- Expected deliverables.*

*- Success criteria.*



*No responsibility shall remain unassigned.*



*---*



*# 4. Responsibility Principles*



*Every responsibility shall satisfy the following principles.*



*- Single ownership.*

*- Explicit authority.*

*- Traceability.*

*- Accountability.*

*- Modularity.*

*- Limited scope.*

*- Governed execution.*

*- Engineering alignment.*



*---*

*Responsibilities are grouped into the following categories.*



*## Governance*



*Engineering standards.*



*---*



*## Architecture*



*System design.*



*---*



*## Development*



*Software implementation.*



*---*



*## Documentation*



*Engineering knowledge.*



*---*



*## Quality Assurance*



*Validation.*



*---*



*## Security*



*Risk mitigation.*



*---*



*## Infrastructure*



*Deployment and operations.*



*---*



*## Product*



*Business alignment.*



*---*



*# 6. Responsibility Model*



*Every responsibility shall define:*



*- Responsibility Identifier.*

*- Responsible Agent.*

*- Scope.*

*- Authority.*

*- Inputs.*

*- Outputs.*

*- Dependencies.*

*- Constraints.*

*- Validation Criteria.*



*Responsibilities shall remain deterministic.*



*---*



*# 7. Authority Levels*



*Authority is divided into four levels.*



*## Level 1 — Execute*



*May perform assigned engineering work.*



*---*



*## Level 2 — Recommend*



*May recommend engineering improvements.*



*---*



*## Level 3 — Coordinate*



*May coordinate multiple agents.*



*---*



*## Level 4 — Govern*



*Reserved for the Project Owner.*



*No AI Agent shall possess governance authority.*



*---*



*# 8. Responsibility Assignment Rules*



*Responsibilities shall be assigned according to the following rules.*



*- One primary owner.*

*- Minimal overlap.*

*- Explicit dependencies.*

*- Documented interfaces.*

*- Approved scope.*

*- Traceable decisions.*



*Responsibilities shall not be duplicated without explicit architectural justification.*



*---*



*# 9. Shared Responsibilities*



*Certain engineering activities require collaboration.*



*Shared responsibilities shall define:*



*- Primary Responsible Agent.*

*- Supporting Agents.*

*- Communication protocol.*

*- Validation ownership.*

*- Completion criteria.*



*Primary ownership remains unique.*

*---*



*# 11. Conflict Resolution*



*Responsibility conflicts shall be resolved using the following priority.*



*1. Governance.*

*2. Architecture.*

*3. Assigned Authority.*

*4. Project Owner.*



*Responsibility conflicts shall never be resolved through autonomous negotiation.*



*---*



*# 12. Responsibility Evolution*



*Responsibilities may evolve when:*



*- Engineering scope changes.*

*- New agents are introduced.*

*- Architecture evolves.*

*- Governance changes.*

*- Engineering maturity increases.*



*All changes require documentation.*



*---*



*# 13. Responsibility Traceability*



*Every engineering artifact shall identify:*



*- Responsible Agent.*

*- Supporting Agents.*

*- Validation Agent.*

*- Approval Authority.*



*Ownership shall remain auditable throughout the engineering lifecycle.*



*---*



*# 14. AI Operational Contract*



*## Responsibilities*



*Every AI Agent shall:*



*- Respect assigned responsibilities.*

*- Operate within authorized scope.*

*- Collaborate through defined protocols.*

*- Produce traceable deliverables.*

*- Escalate responsibility conflicts.*



*---*



*## Authorized Actions*



*Agents may:*



*- Execute assigned work.*

*- Request collaboration.*

*- Delegate approved subtasks.*

*- Recommend responsibility refinements.*

*- Escalate unresolved ownership issues.*



*---*



*## Restricted Actions*



*Agents shall not:*



*- Perform responsibilities owned by another agent without authorization.*

*- Modify responsibility assignments.*

*- Override governance.*

*- Expand scope autonomously.*

*- Ignore responsibility boundaries.*



*---*



*## Success Criteria*



*The Responsibility Matrix is successful when:*



*- Every engineering activity has one primary owner.*

*- Collaboration remains efficient.*

*- Responsibility conflicts are minimal.*

*- Engineering quality improves.*

*- Governance remains intact.*



*---*



*## Failure Conditions*



*The Responsibility Matrix fails when:*



*- Responsibilities overlap.*

*- Ownership is ambiguous.*

*- Agents exceed authority.*

*- Engineering artifacts lack ownership.*

*- Governance boundaries are violated.*



*---*



*## Escalation*



*Responsibility conflicts affecting architecture, governance, or engineering quality shall be escalated to the Project Owner before execution continues.*



*---*



*# 15. Governance*



*The Agent Responsibilities Matrix governs the allocation of engineering responsibilities across every AI Agent within the ecosystem.*



*Future agents shall receive explicit responsibilities before entering operational status.*



*No AI Agent may execute outside the responsibility model established herein.*
