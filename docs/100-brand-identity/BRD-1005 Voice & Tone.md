*# 1. Purpose*



*This document defines the Voice and Tone standards for the Francisco Sánchez Portfolio.*



*Voice represents the consistent personality expressed through written communication.*



*Tone defines how that voice adapts according to context while preserving the same identity.*



*Together, they ensure that every piece of written content communicates professionalism, technical credibility, and authenticity.*



*---*



*# 2. Voice Statement*



*The portfolio speaks with the voice of a professional software engineer.*



*Its communication is:*



*- Clear.*

*- Honest.*

*- Technical when necessary.*

*- Accessible whenever possible.*

*- Respectful.*

*- Evidence-driven.*

*- Calm.*

*- Confident without arrogance.*



*The voice remains consistent regardless of the communication channel.*



*---*



*# 3. Voice Characteristics*



*## Professional*



*Communication reflects maturity, discipline, and respect.*



*The portfolio avoids slang, exaggerated expressions, and unnecessary informality.*



*---*



*## Clear*



*Ideas are expressed in a direct and understandable manner.*



*Technical terminology is used only when it improves precision.*



*Whenever possible, concepts should be explained without unnecessary complexity.*



*---*



*## Honest*



*Claims must be truthful and supported by evidence.*



*Unknowns, limitations, and future work should be acknowledged transparently.*



*Professional credibility is more valuable than appearing perfect.*



*---*



*## Technical*



*Engineering concepts are explained accurately.*



*Technical depth should demonstrate understanding rather than showcase complexity.*



*---*



*## Educational*



*The portfolio seeks to help readers understand engineering decisions.*



*Whenever appropriate, explanations should provide context instead of only presenting conclusions.*



*---*



*## Respectful*



*Communication should demonstrate respect for readers, collaborators, clients, and the broader software engineering community.*



*---*



*## Consistent*



*The same writing style shall be maintained across all documents and communication channels.*



*Consistency is considered a quality attribute.*



*---*



*# 4. Tone Adaptation*



*While the Voice remains constant, the Tone adapts to the communication context.*



*## Technical Documentation*



*Tone:*



*- Precise.*

*- Structured.*

*- Objective.*

*- Detailed.*



*Objective:*



*Communicate engineering decisions with maximum clarity.*



*---*



*## Case Studies*



*Tone:*



*- Analytical.*

*- Reflective.*

*- Educational.*



*Objective:*



*Explain both the technical solution and the reasoning behind it.*



*---*



*## Portfolio Pages*



*Tone:*



*- Professional.*

*- Welcoming.*

*- Confident.*



*Objective:*



*Create trust while maintaining technical credibility.*



*---*



*## Blog Articles*



*Tone:*



*- Conversational.*

*- Educational.*

*- Practical.*



*Objective:*



*Share knowledge without sacrificing technical accuracy.*



*---*



*## Contact Page*



*Tone:*



*- Friendly.*

*- Professional.*

*- Approachable.*



*Objective:*



*Encourage communication while maintaining professionalism.*



*---*



*## Error Messages*



*Tone:*



*- Calm.*

*- Helpful.*

*- Human.*



*Objective:*



*Help users recover without creating frustration.*



*---*



*# 5. Writing Principles*



*Every written artifact should:*



*- Prioritize clarity.*

*- Eliminate ambiguity.*

*- Avoid unnecessary repetition.*

*- Use active voice whenever practical.*

*- Present ideas logically.*

*- Explain technical decisions.*

*- Remain concise without omitting important information.*



*Writing quality reflects engineering quality.*



*---*



*# 6. Language Guidelines*



*Preferred language characteristics include:*



*- Short, well-structured sentences.*

*- Precise terminology.*

*- Consistent vocabulary.*

*- Neutral, professional wording.*

*- Minimal marketing language.*



*Avoid vague or generic expressions that do not add information.*



*---*



*# 7. Expressions to Encourage*



*The portfolio should naturally use expressions such as:*



*- Designed.*

*- Implemented.*

*- Documented.*

*- Improved.*

*- Evaluated.*

*- Optimized.*

*- Integrated.*

*- Validated.*

*- Measured.*

*- Demonstrated.*



*These verbs communicate action and ownership.*



*---*



*# 8. Expressions to Avoid*



*Avoid language that weakens credibility, including:*



*- "Passionate about..."*

*- "Rockstar developer."*

*- "Ninja programmer."*

*- "Guru."*

*- "Best-in-class."*

*- "World-class."*

*- "Revolutionary."*

*- "Game-changing."*

*- "Cutting-edge" (without evidence).*



*Marketing clichés should never replace technical substance.*



*---*



*# 9. Consistency Rules*



*Voice and Tone shall remain consistent across:*



*- Website.*

*- Documentation.*

*- GitHub repositories.*

*- README files.*

*- Resume.*

*- LinkedIn.*

*- Technical presentations.*

*- Case studies.*

*- Blog posts.*

*- Professional communications.*



*Consistency strengthens recognition and trust.*



*---*



*# 10. Governance*



*This document defines the official communication standards for the portfolio.*



*Future updates shall preserve the established voice while allowing tone refinements as the project evolves.*



*---*



*# 11. Compliance*



*All written content associated with the portfolio shall comply with the Voice and Tone standards defined herein.*



*Any significant deviation shall be reviewed and documented before publication.*
