*# 1. Purpose*



*This document defines the mandatory engineering review process that every AI-assisted engineering activity shall complete before being considered finished.*



*Engineering Review ensures that every deliverable satisfies the project's quality standards, governance policies, architectural principles, documentation requirements, and implementation objectives.*



*No engineering deliverable shall be considered complete without a successful review.*



*---*



*# 2. Objectives*



*Engineering Review aims to:*



*- Verify engineering quality.*

*- Ensure governance compliance.*

*- Detect defects early.*

*- Validate architectural consistency.*

*- Improve maintainability.*

*- Reduce technical debt.*

*- Preserve repository integrity.*

*- Guarantee delivery readiness.*



*---*



*# 3. Engineering Review Philosophy*



*Review is a validation activity, not an approval ceremony.*



*Every engineering artifact shall demonstrate compliance with project standards before acceptance.*



*Review focuses on engineering correctness rather than personal preference.*



*---*



*# 4. Review Scope*



*Engineering Review applies to:*



*- Source code.*

*- Documentation.*

*- Architecture.*

*- Configuration.*

*- Infrastructure.*

*- Design artifacts.*

*- Prompt libraries.*

*- AI agents.*

*- Cursor Rules.*

*- Repository structure.*



*Every engineering artifact shall be reviewable.*



*---*



*# 5. Review Workflow*



*Every review shall follow the sequence below.*



*1. Identify deliverables.*

*2. Validate scope.*

*3. Execute technical review.*

*4. Validate architecture.*

*5. Validate documentation.*

*6. Validate governance.*

*7. Validate quality gates.*

*8. Record findings.*

*9. Produce review outcome.*



*Review concludes only after all mandatory validations succeed.*



*---*



*# 6. Review Categories*



*Engineering reviews shall include:*



*## Technical Review*



*Evaluates implementation quality.*



*---*



*## Architecture Review*



*Evaluates architectural compliance.*



*---*



*## Documentation Review*



*Evaluates documentation completeness and synchronization.*



*---*



*## Governance Review*



*Evaluates compliance with engineering standards.*



*---*



*## Quality Review*



*Evaluates maintainability, consistency, and overall engineering quality.*



*---*



*# 7. Review Criteria*



*Every engineering review shall evaluate:*



*- Correctness.*

*- Consistency.*

*- Simplicity.*

*- Maintainability.*

*- Scalability.*

*- Performance.*

*- Security.*

*- Accessibility.*

*- Documentation.*

*- Traceability.*



*---*



*# 8. Review Findings*



*Findings shall be classified as:*



*## Critical*



*Execution cannot continue.*



*---*



*## Major*



*Correction required before approval.*



*---*



*## Minor*



*Improvement recommended.*



*---*



*## Observation*



*Informational recommendation.*



*---*



*# 9. Acceptance Criteria*



*Engineering work may be accepted only when:*



*- All critical findings are resolved.*

*- All major findings are resolved.*

*- Quality gates pass.*

*- Documentation is synchronized.*

*- Governance compliance is verified.*



*Acceptance requires completion of every mandatory criterion.*



*---*



*# 10. Review Traceability*



*Every review shall record:*



*- Reviewed artifacts.*

*- Reviewer.*

*- Review date.*

*- Findings.*

*- Resolutions.*

*- Final decision.*



*Review history shall remain permanently traceable.*



*---*



*# 11. Continuous Improvement*



*Review outcomes shall contribute to:*



*- Engineering standards.*

*- Cursor Rules.*

*- AI Governance.*

*- Development workflows.*

*- Quality metrics.*



*Lessons learned shall continuously improve the engineering process.*



*---*



*# 12. AI Operational Contract*



*## Responsibilities*



*Cursor shall:*



*- Execute engineering reviews.*

*- Detect defects.*

*- Validate compliance.*

*- Produce review findings.*

*- Recommend improvements.*



*---*



*## Authorized Actions*



*Cursor may:*



*- Reject non-compliant deliverables.*

*- Request corrections.*

*- Recommend refactoring.*

*- Suggest documentation updates.*

*- Escalate unresolved findings.*



*---*



*## Restricted Actions*



*Cursor shall not:*



*- Approve incomplete work.*

*- Ignore quality gates.*

*- Skip mandatory reviews.*

*- Override governance.*

*- Accept unresolved critical findings.*



*---*



*## Success Criteria*



*Engineering Review is successful when:*



*- All review stages complete.*

*- Mandatory findings are resolved.*

*- Documentation is synchronized.*

*- Governance compliance is verified.*

*- Deliverables satisfy acceptance criteria.*



*---*



*## Failure Conditions*



*Engineering Review fails when:*



*- Critical findings remain unresolved.*

*- Governance is violated.*

*- Documentation is incomplete.*

*- Quality gates fail.*

*- Acceptance criteria are not satisfied.*



*---*



*## Escalation*



*Persistent review failures shall be escalated to the Project Owner before engineering work is approved.*



*---*



*# 13. Governance*



*Engineering Review is mandatory for every AI-assisted engineering deliverable.*



*Future Cursor Rules, AI Agents, Prompt Libraries, and Development Workflows shall incorporate the review process defined herein unless superseded by a newer approved standard.*
