*# 1. Purpose*



*This document defines the official Certifications content specification for the Francisco Sánchez Portfolio.*



*It serves as the authoritative source of truth for all professional certifications, certificates, credentials, licenses, and verified learning achievements presented throughout the portfolio.*



*Every published certification shall derive from this document.*



*---*



*# 2. Objectives*



*The Certifications content aims to:*



*- Present verified professional credentials.*

*- Demonstrate continuous learning.*

*- Support recruiter evaluation.*

*- Validate technical competencies.*

*- Improve professional credibility.*

*- Enable structured content reuse.*

*- Maintain consistency across all portfolio platforms.*



*---*



*# 3. Content Philosophy*



*Certifications represent externally validated knowledge.*



*Only certifications, credentials, licenses, and learning achievements that can be verified through official issuers or supporting evidence shall be included.*



*The portfolio shall distinguish clearly between completed certifications, active certifications, expired certifications, and ongoing learning programs.*



*---*



*# 4. Target Audience*



*The Certifications content is intended for:*



*- Technical recruiters.*

*- Hiring managers.*

*- Engineering managers.*

*- Software architects.*

*- Potential clients.*

*- Professional collaborators.*

*- Technical interviewers.*



*---*



*# 5. Certification Structure*



*Each certification shall include the following information.*



*| Section | Purpose |*

*|----------|---------|*

*| Certification Name | Official certification title. |*

*| Issuing Organization | Institution granting the certification. |*

*| Category | Technical domain or knowledge area. |*

*| Description | Brief summary of the certification. |*

*| Issue Date | Certification issue date. |*

*| Expiration Date | Expiration date (if applicable). |*

*| Credential ID | Official identifier (when available). |*

*| Verification URL | Public verification link (when available). |*

*| Related Skills | Competencies validated. |*

*| Related Projects | Portfolio projects supported by the certification. |*



*The structure shall remain consistent across all certification entries.*



*---*



*# 6. Certification Attributes*



*Each certification shall include, at minimum:*



*- Certification Identifier.*

*- Official Name.*

*- Issuing Organization.*

*- Category.*

*- Status.*

*- Issue Date.*

*- Expiration Date.*

*- Credential Identifier.*

*- Verification Method.*

*- Description.*

*- Related Skills.*

*- Related Technologies.*

*- Related Projects.*

*- Supporting Evidence.*

*- Last Reviewed.*



*These attributes ensure consistency and traceability.*



*---*



*# 7. Certification Classification*



*Certifications may be classified into one or more of the following categories.*



*| Category | Description |*

*|----------|-------------|*

*| Software Development | Programming and software engineering. |*

*| Cloud Computing | Cloud platforms and infrastructure. |*

*| DevOps | CI/CD, infrastructure, automation. |*

*| Cybersecurity | Security and risk management. |*

*| Artificial Intelligence | AI, ML and automation. |*

*| Data Engineering | Databases and analytics. |*

*| Project Management | Agile and project leadership. |*

*| UX/UI | User experience and interface design. |*

*| Networking | Network infrastructure and administration. |*

*| Other Professional Credentials | Additional verified learning. |*



*Additional categories may be introduced as professional development evolves.*



*---*



*# 8. Content Sources*



*Certification information shall originate from verified sources, including:*



*- Official certification providers.*

*- Accredited educational institutions.*

*- Professional licensing organizations.*

*- Digital credential platforms.*

*- Official transcripts.*

*- Verified completion records.*



*Unsupported or unverifiable credentials shall not be published.*



*---*



*# 9. Version Control*



*The Certifications content is version-controlled.*



*Updates shall occur whenever:*



*- A new certification is obtained.*

*- A certification expires.*

*- A certification is renewed.*

*- Credential information changes.*

*- Verification methods are updated.*



*Historical certification records shall remain traceable.*



*---*



*# 10. Governance*



*The Certifications content is an official portfolio content asset.*



*Changes require:*



*- Verification of authenticity.*

*- Consistency review.*

*- Alignment with Skills and Professional Experience.*

*- Approval by the Project Owner.*



*Published certifications shall remain synchronized across all portfolio representations.*



*---*



*# 11. Compliance*



*The Certifications content shall comply with:*



*- Brand documentation.*

*- Product documentation.*

*- Skills inventory.*

*- Professional Experience.*

*- Resume content.*

*- Portfolio Projects.*



*Every published certification shall accurately represent the current professional profile.*
