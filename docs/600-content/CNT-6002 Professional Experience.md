*# 1. Purpose*



*This document defines the official Professional Experience content specification for the Francisco Sánchez Portfolio.*



*It serves as the authoritative source of truth for all professional work experience presented throughout the portfolio.*



*Every published representation of employment history, engineering roles, responsibilities, and professional achievements shall derive from this document.*



*---*



*# 2. Objectives*



*The Professional Experience content aims to:*



*- Present employment history consistently.*

*- Demonstrate professional growth.*

*- Showcase engineering responsibilities.*

*- Highlight measurable achievements.*

*- Support recruiter evaluation.*

*- Maintain factual accuracy.*

*- Enable content reuse across multiple platforms.*



*---*



*# 3. Content Philosophy*



*Professional experience shall communicate engineering impact rather than merely listing job titles.*



*Every position shall emphasize:*



*- Problems solved.*

*- Responsibilities assumed.*

*- Technologies applied.*

*- Business value delivered.*

*- Engineering decisions.*

*- Professional growth.*



*Descriptions shall be objective, evidence-based, and technically accurate.*



*---*



*# 4. Target Audience*



*The Professional Experience content is intended for:*



*- Technical recruiters.*

*- Hiring managers.*

*- Engineering managers.*

*- Software architects.*

*- Technical interviewers.*

*- Potential clients.*

*- Professional collaborators.*



*---*



*# 5. Experience Structure*



*Each professional experience shall include the following information.*



*| Section | Purpose |*

*|----------|---------|*

*| Organization | Company or institution. |*

*| Position | Official role or title. |*

*| Employment Type | Full-time, internship, freelance, contract, etc. |*

*| Duration | Start and end dates. |*

*| Location | Geographic location or remote status. |*

*| Professional Summary | High-level description of the role. |*

*| Responsibilities | Primary engineering responsibilities. |*

*| Achievements | Measurable accomplishments. |*

*| Technologies | Technologies applied during the role. |*

*| Related Projects | Portfolio projects associated with the experience. |*



*The structure shall remain consistent for every experience entry.*



*---*



*# 6. Experience Attributes*



*Each professional experience shall include, at minimum:*



*- Experience Identifier.*

*- Organization.*

*- Position.*

*- Employment Type.*

*- Start Date.*

*- End Date.*

*- Current Status.*

*- Location.*

*- Industry.*

*- Description.*

*- Responsibilities.*

*- Achievements.*

*- Technologies.*

*- Skills Demonstrated.*

*- Related Projects.*

*- Supporting Evidence.*

*- Last Reviewed.*



*These attributes ensure consistency and traceability.*



*---*



*# 7. Content Sources*



*Professional experience shall originate from verified sources, including:*



*- Employment history.*

*- Internship records.*

*- Freelance engagements.*

*- Consulting work.*

*- Academic professional practice.*

*- Employment contracts (when applicable).*

*- Publicly verifiable professional profiles.*



*Information shall accurately represent actual professional experience.*



*---*



*# 8. Version Control*



*Professional Experience is version-controlled.*



*Updates shall occur whenever:*



*- New employment begins.*

*- Employment concludes.*

*- Responsibilities change significantly.*

*- Promotions occur.*

*- New achievements are documented.*

*- Professional information requires correction.*



*Historical revisions shall remain traceable.*



*---*



*# 9. Governance*



*Professional Experience is an official portfolio content asset.*



*Changes require:*



*- Accuracy verification.*

*- Consistency review.*

*- Alignment with Resume content.*

*- Approval by the Project Owner.*



*Published information shall remain synchronized across every platform.*



*---*



*# 10. Compliance*



*Professional Experience shall comply with:*



*- Brand documentation.*

*- Product documentation.*

*- Resume content.*

*- Portfolio projects.*

*- Skills inventory.*

*- Engineering standards.*



*Unsupported or unverifiable claims shall not be published.*
