*# 1. Purpose*



*This document defines the official Resume content specification for the Francisco Sánchez Portfolio.*



*It establishes the structure, governance, and content requirements for every resume generated from the portfolio ecosystem.*



*The Resume is a derived engineering artifact that shall consume information from the authoritative content sources rather than duplicate it.*



*---*



*# 2. Objectives*



*The Resume aims to:*



*- Present a concise professional profile.*

*- Support recruitment processes.*

*- Highlight engineering competencies.*

*- Demonstrate professional experience.*

*- Showcase relevant projects.*

*- Maintain consistency across resume versions.*

*- Enable automated resume generation.*



*---*



*# 3. Resume Philosophy*



*The Resume is a presentation layer of the portfolio.*



*It summarizes the most relevant professional information while preserving consistency with the underlying engineering documentation.*



*No information shall exist exclusively within the Resume.*



*Every published statement shall originate from an approved content source.*



*---*



*# 4. Target Audience*



*The Resume is intended for:*



*- Technical recruiters.*

*- Hiring managers.*

*- Engineering managers.*

*- Software architects.*

*- Technical interviewers.*

*- Potential clients.*



*Different resume variants may be generated for different audiences while maintaining a common source of truth.*



*---*



*# 5. Resume Structure*



*Every resume shall include the following sections.*



*| Section | Purpose |*

*|----------|---------|*

*| Header | Professional identity and contact information. |*

*| Professional Summary | High-level engineering profile. |*

*| Skills | Core technical competencies. |*

*| Professional Experience | Relevant employment history. |*

*| Portfolio Projects | Selected engineering projects. |*

*| Education | Academic background. |*

*| Certifications | Relevant certifications. |*

*| Languages | Spoken and written languages. |*

*| Contact Information | Professional communication channels. |*



*Additional sections may be included when appropriate.*



*---*



*# 6. Content Sources*



*Resume information shall be derived exclusively from approved portfolio content, including:*



*- CNT-6000 About Me*

*- CNT-6001 Skills*

*- CNT-6002 Professional Experience*

*- CNT-6003 Portfolio Projects*

*- CNT-6005 Certifications*



*The Resume shall not introduce information that is absent from its authoritative sources.*



*---*



*# 7. Resume Variants*



*Multiple resume variants may be maintained.*



*Representative variants include:*



*| Variant | Purpose |*

*|----------|---------|*

*| General Resume | Standard professional profile. |*

*| Frontend Resume | Frontend-focused opportunities. |*

*| Backend Resume | Backend-focused opportunities. |*

*| Full Stack Resume | Full-stack engineering roles. |*

*| Software Architecture Resume | Architecture-oriented positions. |*

*| AI \& Automation Resume | Artificial Intelligence and Automation roles. |*



*All variants shall derive from the same canonical content.*



*---*



*# 8. Version Control*



*The Resume is version-controlled.*



*Updates shall occur whenever:*



*- Professional experience changes.*

*- Skills evolve.*

*- New projects are completed.*

*- Certifications are obtained.*

*- Career objectives are updated.*



*Historical resume versions shall remain traceable.*



*---*



*# 9. Governance*



*The Resume is an official portfolio artifact.*



*Modifications require:*



*- Content consistency verification.*

*- Alignment with Brand documentation.*

*- Validation against authoritative content sources.*

*- Approval by the Project Owner.*



*Published resumes shall remain synchronized with the portfolio.*



*---*



*# 10. Compliance*



*The Resume shall comply with:*



*- Brand documentation.*

*- Product documentation.*

*- Engineering documentation.*

*- Professional Experience.*

*- Skills inventory.*

*- Portfolio Projects.*



*The Resume shall accurately represent the current professional profile.*
