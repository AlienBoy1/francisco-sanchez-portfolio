*# 1. Purpose*



*This document defines the content specification for the "About Me" section of the Francisco Sánchez Portfolio.*



*It establishes the official source of truth for the professional profile presented throughout the portfolio, ensuring consistency across all user interfaces, downloadable assets, and future communication channels.*



*---*



*# 2. Objectives*



*The About Me content aims to:*



*- Introduce the portfolio owner professionally.*

*- Communicate technical identity.*

*- Present engineering philosophy.*

*- Establish credibility.*

*- Build trust with recruiters and clients.*

*- Provide a concise professional narrative.*

*- Maintain consistency across every platform.*



*---*



*# 3. Content Philosophy*



*The About Me section shall communicate who the engineer is, what problems they solve, and how they approach software engineering.*



*Content shall prioritize authenticity, technical depth, and clarity over self-promotion.*



*Claims shall be evidence-based and supported by projects, experience, or documented competencies.*



*---*



*# 4. Target Audience*



*The primary audience includes:*



*- Technical recruiters.*

*- Hiring managers.*

*- Engineering leaders.*

*- Software architects.*

*- Technical interviewers.*

*- Potential clients.*

*- Collaborators.*

*- Open-source contributors.*



*Secondary audiences may include students, educators, and members of the software engineering community.*



*---*



*# 5. Content Structure*



*The About Me content shall be organized into the following sections.*



*| Section | Purpose |*

*|----------|---------|*

*| Professional Introduction | Brief overview of the engineer. |*

*| Professional Summary | High-level technical profile. |*

*| Engineering Philosophy | Personal approach to software engineering. |*

*| Areas of Expertise | Primary technical strengths. |*

*| Technologies | Core technology stack. |*

*| Professional Values | Guiding professional principles. |*

*| Career Goals | Long-term professional aspirations. |*

*| Call to Action | Invitation for collaboration or contact. |*



*The structure shall remain consistent across future revisions.*



*---*



*# 6. Content Requirements*



*The content shall:*



*- Be written in professional English.*

*- Maintain an objective tone.*

*- Be concise yet informative.*

*- Avoid unsupported claims.*

*- Reflect current experience.*

*- Remain technically accurate.*

*- Be updated as the professional profile evolves.*



*---*



*# 7. Content Sources*



*Information included in the About Me section shall originate from approved sources, including:*



*- Professional experience.*

*- Verified technical skills.*

*- Portfolio projects.*

*- Certifications.*

*- Engineering documentation.*

*- Public professional profiles.*

*- Approved resume content.*



*Personal opinions shall be clearly distinguishable from factual information.*



*---*



*# 8. Version Control*



*The About Me content is version-controlled.*



*Changes shall be documented whenever they modify:*



*- Professional summary.*

*- Technical focus.*

*- Areas of expertise.*

*- Career objectives.*

*- Core messaging.*



*Version history shall preserve the evolution of the professional profile.*



*---*



*# 9. Governance*



*The About Me section is an official content asset.*



*Modifications require:*



*- Content review.*

*- Consistency verification.*

*- Alignment with the Brand domain.*

*- Approval by the Project Owner.*



*All published instances shall remain synchronized with this document.*



*---*



*# 10. Compliance*



*The About Me content shall comply with:*



*- Brand documentation.*

*- Product documentation.*

*- Design System.*

*- Accessibility guidelines.*

*- Engineering standards.*



*Content shall remain consistent across every platform where it is published.*
