*# 1. Purpose*



*This document defines the official Skills content specification for the Francisco Sánchez Portfolio.*



*It serves as the authoritative source of truth for all professional competencies, technologies, engineering practices, methodologies, tools, and soft skills presented throughout the portfolio.*



*Every published representation of professional skills shall derive from this document.*



*---*



*# 2. Objectives*



*The Skills content aims to:*



*- Present technical competencies consistently.*

*- Demonstrate engineering capabilities.*

*- Support recruiter evaluation.*

*- Provide structured skill classification.*

*- Maintain accuracy across all portfolio sections.*

*- Facilitate future content reuse.*

*- Ensure long-term maintainability.*



*---*



*# 3. Skills Philosophy*



*Skills represent demonstrated competencies rather than aspirational knowledge.*



*Only technologies, methodologies, tools, and practices supported by practical experience, professional work, academic projects, certifications, or documented learning shall be included.*



*The portfolio shall communicate proficiency honestly and objectively.*



*---*



*# 4. Target Audience*



*The Skills content is intended for:*



*- Technical recruiters.*

*- Hiring managers.*

*- Engineering managers.*

*- Software architects.*

*- Technical interviewers.*

*- Potential clients.*

*- Collaborators.*

*- Open-source communities.*



*---*



*# 5. Skill Categories*



*Professional competencies shall be organized into the following categories.*



*| Category | Description |*

*|----------|-------------|*

*| Programming Languages | Languages used in software development. |*

*| Frontend Development | Client-side technologies and frameworks. |*

*| Backend Development | Server-side technologies and APIs. |*

*| Databases | Relational and NoSQL database systems. |*

*| Software Architecture | Architectural patterns and design principles. |*

*| UI/UX Engineering | User interface implementation and experience. |*

*| DevOps \& Deployment | CI/CD, hosting, infrastructure and automation. |*

*| Cloud Technologies | Cloud platforms and services. |*

*| Testing \& Quality | Testing methodologies and quality assurance. |*

*| Security | Application and infrastructure security. |*

*| Mobile Development | Native and cross-platform mobile technologies. |*

*| Version Control | Source control and collaboration tools. |*

*| AI \& Automation | Artificial Intelligence and workflow automation. |*

*| Project Management | Agile methodologies and planning practices. |*

*| Soft Skills | Professional and interpersonal competencies. |*



*Additional categories may be introduced as the professional profile evolves.*



*---*



*# 6. Skill Attributes*



*Each documented skill shall include, at minimum:*



*- Skill Identifier.*

*- Name.*

*- Category.*

*- Description.*

*- Current Proficiency Level.*

*- Years of Experience (when applicable).*

*- Evidence Source.*

*- Related Projects.*

*- Related Certifications.*

*- Last Reviewed.*



*These attributes ensure consistency and traceability.*



*---*



*# 7. Proficiency Model*



*Every skill shall be evaluated using a standardized proficiency model.*



*Representative levels include:*



*- Fundamental*

*- Intermediate*

*- Advanced*

*- Expert*



*Proficiency shall reflect demonstrated capability rather than subjective confidence.*



*Assessment shall be periodically reviewed as professional experience evolves.*



*---*



*# 8. Content Sources*



*Skill information shall originate from verifiable sources, including:*



*- Professional experience.*

*- Portfolio projects.*

*- Academic work.*

*- Technical certifications.*

*- Open-source contributions.*

*- Engineering documentation.*

*- Continuous learning activities.*



*Unsupported claims shall not be included.*



*---*



*# 9. Version Control*



*The Skills content is version-controlled.*



*Updates shall be documented whenever:*



*- New competencies are acquired.*

*- Existing proficiency changes.*

*- Technologies become obsolete.*

*- Certifications are obtained.*

*- Professional experience expands.*



*Historical revisions shall remain traceable.*



*---*



*# 10. Governance*



*The Skills content is an official content asset.*



*Modifications require:*



*- Technical validation.*

*- Consistency review.*

*- Alignment with portfolio projects.*

*- Approval by the Project Owner.*



*All published skill representations shall remain synchronized with this document.*



*---*



*# 11. Compliance*



*The Skills content shall comply with:*



*- Brand documentation.*

*- Product documentation.*

*- Engineering documentation.*

*- Resume content.*

*- Professional positioning strategy.*



*Published competencies shall accurately represent the current professional profile.*
