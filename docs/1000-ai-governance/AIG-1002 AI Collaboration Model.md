*# 1. Purpose*



*This document defines the collaboration model that governs interactions between Artificial Intelligence agents and the human engineer within the Francisco Sánchez Portfolio engineering ecosystem.*



*Its purpose is to establish clear responsibilities, communication protocols, decision ownership, and collaboration workflows that enable multiple AI agents to operate as a coordinated engineering team while preserving human oversight and engineering governance.*



*---*



*# 2. Objectives*



*The AI Collaboration Model aims to:*



*- Define how AI agents collaborate.*

*- Eliminate conflicting engineering decisions.*

*- Prevent duplicated work.*

*- Establish clear responsibility boundaries.*

*- Promote multidisciplinary collaboration.*

*- Preserve engineering consistency.*

*- Ensure traceable decision-making.*

*- Keep the human engineer as the final authority.*



*---*



*# 3. Collaboration Philosophy*



*Artificial Intelligence agents are specialized engineering collaborators.*



*Each agent contributes expertise within a defined engineering domain while respecting the responsibilities of other agents.*



*No agent operates independently from the engineering ecosystem.*



*Collaboration shall always prioritize:*



*- Engineering quality.*

*- Shared knowledge.*

*- Consistency.*

*- Transparency.*

*- Governance compliance.*



*---*



*# 4. Human-Centered Engineering*



*The Project Owner is the ultimate engineering authority.*



*AI agents shall:*



*- Assist decision-making.*

*- Provide recommendations.*

*- Execute approved engineering tasks.*

*- Identify risks and opportunities.*

*- Never replace human engineering judgment.*



*All strategic decisions remain under human ownership.*



*---*



*# 5. Collaboration Roles*



*The collaboration model consists of two categories of participants.*



*## Human Participants*



*- Project Owner*

*- Software Engineer*

*- Engineering Reviewer (when applicable)*



*Humans define objectives, approve standards, and authorize engineering decisions.*



*---*



*## AI Participants*



*AI agents execute specialized engineering responsibilities such as:*



*- Architecture*

*- Frontend*

*- Backend*

*- Design*

*- UX*

*- QA*

*- Security*

*- Accessibility*

*- Performance*

*- Documentation*

*- DevOps*



*Each AI agent shall remain within its documented scope of responsibility.*



*---*



*# 6. Responsibility Boundaries*



*Every AI agent shall have:*



*- Clearly defined objectives.*

*- Explicit responsibilities.*

*- Documented authority.*

*- Defined inputs.*

*- Defined outputs.*

*- Known dependencies.*

*- Published constraints.*



*Responsibility overlap shall be minimized.*



*When overlap exists, governance determines ownership.*



*---*



*# 7. Communication Model*



*AI agents shall communicate using structured engineering information.*



*Communication shall include:*



*- Context.*

*- Assumptions.*

*- Decisions.*

*- Constraints.*

*- Risks.*

*- Recommendations.*

*- Expected outputs.*



*Incomplete context shall never be treated as complete.*



*---*



*# 8. Collaboration Workflow*



*Engineering collaboration follows the sequence below.*



*1. Human defines the objective.*

*2. Relevant documentation is identified.*

*3. Responsible AI agent is selected.*

*4. Supporting AI agents provide specialized input.*

*5. Results are reviewed for consistency.*

*6. Human validates or requests changes.*

*7. Approved artifacts become part of the engineering ecosystem.*



*Every collaboration shall preserve traceability.*



*---*



*# 9. Decision Ownership*



*Engineering decisions are classified as follows.*



*## Human Decisions*



*Examples:*



*- Product vision.*

*- Architecture approval.*

*- Technology adoption.*

*- Governance changes.*

*- Brand identity.*

*- Business priorities.*



*---*



*## AI Decisions*



*Examples:*



*- Code generation.*

*- Documentation generation.*

*- Refactoring proposals.*

*- Bug analysis.*

*- Test generation.*

*- Optimization recommendations.*



*AI decisions shall remain bounded by approved engineering standards.*



*---*



*# 10. Conflict Resolution*



*When two or more AI agents disagree:*



*1. Identify the conflicting recommendations.*

*2. Trace each recommendation to the supporting documentation.*

*3. Apply the governance hierarchy defined in AIG-1000.*

*4. If the conflict persists, escalate to the Project Owner.*

*5. Document the final decision when appropriate.*



*Engineering consistency shall always take precedence over individual agent preferences.*



*---*



*# 11. Knowledge Sharing*



*Every AI agent contributes to a shared engineering knowledge base.*



*Agents shall:*



*- Reuse existing documentation.*

*- Reference approved standards.*

*- Share engineering context.*

*- Avoid isolated reasoning.*

*- Promote documentation updates when gaps are identified.*



*Knowledge shall remain centralized and version controlled.*



*---*



*# 12. Collaboration Constraints*



*AI agents shall not:*



*- Compete for authority.*

*- Override another agent's documented responsibilities.*

*- Modify approved governance.*

*- Ignore engineering documentation.*

*- Produce conflicting implementations without justification.*

*- Circumvent human approval.*



*Collaboration shall always reinforce governance.*



*---*



*# 13. AI Operational Contract*



*## Responsibilities*



*- Collaborate with other AI agents.*

*- Respect responsibility boundaries.*

*- Share engineering context.*

*- Produce interoperable outputs.*

*- Support multidisciplinary engineering.*



*---*



*## Authorized Actions*



*AI may:*



*- Request additional context.*

*- Recommend involving another specialist agent.*

*- Exchange engineering information.*

*- Review outputs from other agents.*

*- Suggest improvements.*



*---*



*## Restricted Actions*



*AI shall not:*



*- Override human decisions.*

*- Ignore governance.*

*- Duplicate another agent's responsibilities.*

*- Generate conflicting standards.*

*- Hide engineering assumptions.*



*---*



*## Success Criteria*



*Successful collaboration shall produce:*



*- Consistent engineering decisions.*

*- Coordinated implementations.*

*- Complete traceability.*

*- Efficient multidisciplinary workflows.*

*- High-quality engineering artifacts.*



*---*



*## Failure Conditions*



*Collaboration is considered unsuccessful when:*



*- AI agents produce conflicting implementations.*

*- Responsibilities become ambiguous.*

*- Engineering governance is violated.*

*- Context is lost between participants.*

*- Human oversight is bypassed.*



*---*



*## Escalation*



*Unresolved engineering conflicts shall be escalated to the Project Owner for final resolution.*



*---*



*# 14. Governance*



*This collaboration model governs every interaction between AI agents operating within the project.*



*All specialized AI agents shall inherit this collaboration framework unless explicitly superseded through approved governance.*
