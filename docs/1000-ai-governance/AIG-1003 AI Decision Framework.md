*# 1. Purpose*



*This document defines the engineering decision framework that every Artificial Intelligence agent shall follow before producing any artifact within the Francisco Sánchez Portfolio ecosystem.*



*The objective is to ensure that every decision is deterministic, documented, traceable, explainable, and fully aligned with the project's engineering governance.*



*The framework standardizes reasoning independently of the AI provider, execution environment, or specialization of the agent.*



*---*



*# 2. Objectives*



*The AI Decision Framework aims to:*



*- Standardize engineering reasoning.*

*- Eliminate arbitrary implementation choices.*

*- Reduce inconsistency between AI agents.*

*- Increase engineering predictability.*

*- Improve traceability.*

*- Promote evidence-based decisions.*

*- Preserve architectural integrity.*

*- Facilitate future automation.*



*---*



*# 3. Decision Philosophy*



*Engineering decisions shall never originate from intuition alone.*



*Every decision shall be supported by:*



*- Approved documentation.*

*- Available project context.*

*- Existing implementation.*

*- Engineering best practices compatible with the documented standards.*



*Whenever sufficient evidence is unavailable, the agent shall request clarification rather than inventing permanent decisions.*



*---*



*# 4. Decision Hierarchy*



*Every engineering decision shall be evaluated according to the following hierarchy.*



*1. Governance (GOV)*

*2. AI Governance (AIG)*

*3. Brand (BRD)*

*4. Product (PRD)*

*5. Design System (DS)*

*6. Architecture (ARC)*

*7. Development (DEV)*

*8. Deployment (DEP)*

*9. Content (CNT)*

*10. Case Studies (CS)*

*11. Current task requirements*



*Higher-priority documentation always overrides lower-priority guidance.*



*---*



*# 5. Engineering Decision Cycle*



*Every AI agent shall execute the following sequence before producing an output.*



*## Step 1 — Understand*



*Understand the engineering objective.*



*Identify:*



*- Desired outcome.*

*- Scope.*

*- Constraints.*

*- Expected deliverable.*



*---*



*## Step 2 — Gather Context*



*Collect every applicable source of information.*



*Sources may include:*



*- Engineering documentation.*

*- Existing implementation.*

*- Repository structure.*

*- Project conventions.*

*- User instructions.*



*No decision shall be made without sufficient context.*



*---*



*## Step 3 — Validate*



*Verify consistency between:*



*- Documentation.*

*- Architecture.*

*- Existing implementation.*

*- Requested task.*



*Conflicts shall be resolved before implementation begins.*



*---*



*## Step 4 — Evaluate Alternatives*



*Whenever multiple valid solutions exist, AI shall compare them using objective engineering criteria.*



*Evaluation shall consider:*



*- Simplicity.*

*- Maintainability.*

*- Performance.*

*- Security.*

*- Accessibility.*

*- Scalability.*

*- Documentation consistency.*



*---*



*## Step 5 — Select*



*Choose the alternative that best satisfies documented engineering standards.*



*Selection shall prioritize long-term maintainability over short-term convenience.*



*---*



*## Step 6 — Verify*



*Before delivering the result, AI shall verify:*



*- Architecture compliance.*

*- Design System compliance.*

*- Naming consistency.*

*- Coding standards.*

*- Documentation consistency.*

*- Repository organization.*



*---*



*## Step 7 — Deliver*



*Produce the final artifact.*



*The output shall be deterministic, reproducible, and compatible with the engineering ecosystem.*



*---*



*# 6. Decision Principles*



*Every engineering decision shall satisfy the following principles.*



*## Evidence First*



*Recommendations shall be supported by evidence.*



*---*



*## Consistency Over Creativity*



*Consistency with the project is more valuable than novel solutions.*



*---*



*## Simplicity Before Complexity*



*The simplest maintainable solution shall be preferred.*



*---*



*## Reuse Before Reinvention*



*Existing implementations shall be reused whenever appropriate.*



*---*



*## Prevention Before Correction*



*Potential issues shall be prevented whenever possible instead of corrected afterward.*



*---*



*## Documentation Before Assumption*



*Missing documentation shall be identified rather than replaced with assumptions.*



*---*



*# 7. Decision Categories*



*Engineering decisions are classified into four categories.*



*## Strategic*



*Examples:*



*- Product direction.*

*- Architecture evolution.*

*- Governance modifications.*



*Approval required:*



*Project Owner.*



*---*



*## Tactical*



*Examples:*



*- Component organization.*

*- API design.*

*- Folder structure.*



*Approval required:*



*Engineering documentation compliance.*



*---*



*## Operational*



*Examples:*



*- Code generation.*

*- Refactoring.*

*- Test generation.*

*- Documentation updates.*



*Approval required:*



*Automatic when compliant with governance.*



*---*



*## Informational*



*Examples:*



*- Recommendations.*

*- Observations.*

*- Risk identification.*



*Approval required:*



*None.*



*---*



*# 8. Conflict Management*



*When conflicting information exists, AI shall:*



*1. Detect the conflict.*

*2. Identify every conflicting source.*

*3. Apply the documented hierarchy.*

*4. Explain the selected interpretation.*

*5. Recommend documentation improvements if necessary.*



*Engineering conflicts shall never be silently ignored.*



*---*



*# 9. Decision Logging*



*Significant engineering decisions should be documented whenever they:*



*- Affect architecture.*

*- Modify standards.*

*- Introduce new patterns.*

*- Change workflows.*

*- Impact future maintainability.*



*Documented reasoning improves long-term traceability.*



*---*



*# 10. AI Operational Contract*



*## Responsibilities*



*- Apply structured engineering reasoning.*

*- Preserve deterministic decision-making.*

*- Validate every implementation.*

*- Identify documentation conflicts.*

*- Recommend improvements.*



*---*



*## Authorized Actions*



*AI may:*



*- Evaluate alternatives.*

*- Recommend implementations.*

*- Reject inconsistent requests.*

*- Suggest documentation updates.*

*- Escalate unresolved conflicts.*



*---*



*## Restricted Actions*



*AI shall not:*



*- Skip the decision cycle.*

*- Ignore higher-priority documentation.*

*- Invent undocumented standards.*

*- Hide reasoning when justification is required.*

*- Produce conflicting engineering outputs intentionally.*



*---*



*## Success Criteria*



*A successful engineering decision shall be:*



*- Documented.*

*- Traceable.*

*- Consistent.*

*- Explainable.*

*- Maintainable.*

*- Reproducible.*



*---*



*## Failure Conditions*



*Decision-making is considered unsuccessful when:*



*- Documentation is ignored.*

*- Conflicts remain unresolved.*

*- Engineering consistency is violated.*

*- Outputs cannot be justified.*

*- Decisions become non-reproducible.*



*---*



*## Escalation*



*When evidence is insufficient, AI shall request clarification from the Project Owner before continuing.*



*---*



*# 11. Governance*



*Every AI agent operating within this engineering ecosystem shall execute the decision framework defined in this document before producing any engineering artifact.*



*Specialized agents may extend this framework but shall never weaken or bypass it.*
