*# 1. Purpose*



*This document serves as the official engineering case study for the Altus Gym project.*



*It documents the complete engineering journey of the project, including the problem domain, business objectives, design decisions, architectural evolution, implementation strategy, engineering challenges, and lessons learned.*



*This case study complements the project's technical documentation by explaining not only what was built, but why each engineering decision was made.*



*---*



*# 2. Project Overview*



*Altus Gym is a software project developed to modernize gym management through a web-based platform focused on usability, scalability, and operational efficiency.*



*The project explores the design and implementation of a modern software architecture capable of supporting future growth while delivering an intuitive experience for both administrators and gym members.*



*---*



*# 3. Problem Statement*



*Traditional gym management often relies on fragmented tools, manual processes, and disconnected information systems.*



*These limitations reduce operational efficiency, complicate member management, and hinder business growth.*



*The project was conceived to investigate how modern web technologies and software engineering practices can improve the management experience for fitness businesses.*



*---*



*# 4. Objectives*



*The project pursues the following objectives.*



*- Improve operational efficiency.*

*- Centralize business information.*

*- Simplify administrative workflows.*

*- Enhance user experience.*

*- Support future scalability.*

*- Demonstrate modern engineering practices.*

*- Build a maintainable software architecture.*
