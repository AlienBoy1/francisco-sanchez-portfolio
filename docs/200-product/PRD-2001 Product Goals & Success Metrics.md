*# 1. Purpose*



*This document defines the strategic goals of the Francisco Sánchez Portfolio and establishes the metrics used to evaluate whether the product successfully fulfills its purpose.*



*Goals define what the product intends to achieve.*



*Success Metrics define how achievement will be measured.*



*Every future product decision should contribute toward one or more goals defined in this document.*



*---*



*# 2. Product Goals*



*The portfolio pursues the following strategic goals.*



*## Goal 1*



*Demonstrate professional software engineering capability.*



*---*



*## Goal 2*



*Communicate technical knowledge through real-world implementations rather than theoretical descriptions.*



*---*



*## Goal 3*



*Build trust through documentation, transparency, and engineering discipline.*



*---*



*## Goal 4*



*Provide recruiters and engineering leaders with sufficient technical evidence to evaluate professional capabilities.*



*---*



*## Goal 5*



*Serve as a long-term engineering platform capable of evolving throughout the owner's career.*



*---*



*## Goal 6*



*Document architectural thinking and engineering decision-making processes.*



*---*



*## Goal 7*



*Maintain production-level software quality.*



*---*



*## Goal 8*



*Become the single source of truth for professional work.*



*---*



*# 3. Success Principles*



*Success shall be evaluated using measurable indicators whenever practical.*



*Measurements should emphasize product quality and engineering value rather than popularity.*



*Metrics shall support decision-making rather than vanity.*



*---*



*# 4. Strategic Success Metrics*



*The following metrics evaluate the product as a whole.*



*| Metric | Target |*

*|----------|---------|*

*| Engineering documentation completed | 100% |*

*| Core product features implemented | 100% |*

*| Broken internal links | 0 |*

*| Accessibility compliance | WCAG AA or higher |*

*| Lighthouse Performance | ≥95 |*

*| Lighthouse Accessibility | ≥95 |*

*| Lighthouse Best Practices | ≥95 |*

*| Lighthouse SEO | ≥95 |*

*| Build failures on main branch | 0 |*

*| Critical production bugs | 0 |*



*These metrics define the expected engineering quality of the portfolio.*



*---*



*# 5. User Experience Metrics*



*The product should provide a high-quality user experience.*



*Examples include:*



*- Fast page loading.*

*- Clear navigation.*

*- Responsive design.*

*- Accessible interfaces.*

*- Minimal interaction friction.*

*- Consistent user interface.*

*- Predictable behavior.*



*User experience should prioritize clarity over visual complexity.*



*---*



*# 6. Professional Impact Metrics*



*The portfolio should generate measurable professional value.*



*Examples include:*



*- Recruiter engagement.*

*- Technical interview invitations.*

*- Professional referrals.*

*- Collaboration opportunities.*

*- Client inquiries.*

*- Engineering networking opportunities.*



*These indicators evaluate professional effectiveness rather than website popularity.*



*---*



*# 7. Technical Quality Metrics*



*Engineering quality shall be continuously monitored.*



*Examples include:*



*- Test coverage.*

*- Static analysis results.*

*- Linting compliance.*

*- TypeScript strict compliance.*

*- Performance budgets.*

*- Accessibility audits.*

*- Documentation completeness.*

*- Architecture review compliance.*



*Quality metrics shall be integrated into the development workflow whenever possible.*



*---*



*# 8. Documentation Metrics*



*Documentation quality is considered a first-class product objective.*



*Success indicators include:*



*- Every implemented feature documented.*

*- Every architecture decision traceable.*

*- Every document versioned.*

*- References validated.*

*- No orphan documents.*

*- Documentation index updated.*

*- Cross-references maintained.*



*Documentation should evolve alongside implementation.*



*---*



*# 9. Long-Term Success Indicators*



*The product should remain valuable throughout multiple years.*



*Indicators include:*



*- Easy maintainability.*

*- Sustainable architecture.*

*- Modular implementation.*

*- Technology adaptability.*

*- Documentation continuity.*

*- Consistent engineering standards.*



*Longevity is considered part of product success.*



*---*



*# 10. Metrics Review Process*



*Success Metrics should be reviewed:*



*- At major milestones.*

*- Before production releases.*

*- During architecture reviews.*

*- Following significant feature additions.*

*- During annual portfolio reviews.*



*Metrics should evolve only when justified by product maturity.*



*---*



*# 11. Governance*



*The goals and metrics defined herein govern product evaluation throughout the project lifecycle.*



*Future objectives may be added provided they remain aligned with the Product Vision.*



*---*



*# 12. Compliance*



*Every significant product initiative should support at least one Product Goal defined in this document.*



*New features lacking alignment with established goals should be reconsidered before implementation.*
