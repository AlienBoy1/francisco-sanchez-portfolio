*# 1. Purpose*



*This document defines the long-term evolution strategy for the Francisco Sánchez Portfolio.*



*The Product Roadmap establishes the planned progression of product capabilities while ensuring alignment with the project's vision, engineering standards, and long-term objectives.*



*The roadmap is strategic rather than calendar-driven.*



*---*



*# 2. Roadmap Philosophy*



*The portfolio shall evolve incrementally through clearly defined capability milestones.*



*Each phase shall*



*- Deliver measurable value.*

*- Improve the professional experience.*

*- Strengthen engineering quality.*

*- Remain independently releasable.*

*- Build upon previous capabilities without introducing unnecessary complexity.*



*The roadmap prioritizes sustainable growth over rapid expansion.*



*---*



*# 3. Strategic Objectives*



*The roadmap supports the following strategic objectives*



*- Build a world-class engineering portfolio.*

*- Demonstrate software engineering excellence.*

*- Showcase real-world projects.*

*- Publish engineering knowledge.*

*- Support long-term professional growth.*

*- Maintain a scalable technical platform.*



*Every roadmap phase shall contribute to at least one strategic objective.*



*---*

*# 7. Roadmap Governance*



*The roadmap is a living engineering artifact.*



*Changes shall*



*- Preserve strategic alignment.*

*- Maintain traceability.*

*- Be documented through governance processes.*

*- Avoid disrupting completed phases.*



*Major roadmap changes shall be reviewed before implementation.*



*---*



*# 8. Success Indicators*



*Roadmap progress shall be evaluated using indicators such as*



*- Product capability completion.*

*- Documentation coverage.*

*- Engineering quality metrics.*

*- Accessibility compliance.*

*- Performance benchmarks.*

*- Recruiter engagement.*

*- Portfolio usability.*

*- Technical content publication.*



*Indicators shall evolve alongside the product.*



*---*



*# 9. Risks*



*Potential roadmap risks include*



*- Scope expansion.*

*- Documentation drift.*

*- Technical debt.*

*- Architectural inconsistency.*

*- Delayed feature delivery.*

*- Technology obsolescence.*



*Risks shall be monitored throughout the project lifecycle.*



*---*



*# 10. Governance*



*Every roadmap revision shall*



*- Preserve completed milestones.*

*- Maintain document traceability.*

*- Update affected documentation.*

*- Record significant changes through the governance process.*



*The roadmap shall remain consistent with the Product Vision.*



*---*



*# 11. Compliance*



*Implementation priorities shall follow the roadmap unless an approved governance decision explicitly authorizes deviation.*



*Roadmap deviations shall be documented to preserve planning transparency.*
