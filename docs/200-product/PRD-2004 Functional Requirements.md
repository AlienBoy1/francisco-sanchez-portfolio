*# 1. Purpose*



*This document defines the functional requirements of the Francisco Sánchez Portfolio.*



*Functional Requirements describe what the product shall do from the user's perspective.*



*Each requirement shall be uniquely identifiable, testable, and traceable throughout the project lifecycle.*



*---*



*# 2. Requirement Philosophy*



*Every functional requirement shall:*



*- Deliver user value.*

*- Be implementation independent.*

*- Be objectively verifiable.*

*- Have a unique identifier.*

*- Support at least one Product Goal.*

*- Support one or more User Journeys.*



*Requirements describe expected behavior, not implementation details.*



*---*



*# 3. Functional Areas*



*The product is divided into the following functional areas.*



*- Homepage*

*- About*

*- Skills*

*- Experience*

*- Projects*

*- Case Studies*

*- Documentation*

*- Blog*

*- Resume*

*- Contact*

*- Navigation*

*- Search*

*- Analytics*



*Each area will be expanded throughout the project.*



*---*



*# 4. Homepage Requirements*



*| ID | Requirement |*

*|-----|-------------|*

*| FR-0001 | The system shall present a professional landing page. |*

*| FR-0002 | The landing page shall communicate the professional positioning within five seconds. |*

*| FR-0003 | The homepage shall provide clear calls to action. |*

*| FR-0004 | The homepage shall provide direct access to major portfolio sections. |*

*| FR-0005 | The homepage shall remain fully responsive across supported devices. |*

*# 5. Navigation Requirements*



*| ID | Requirement |*

*|-----|-------------|*

*| FR-0012 | Navigation shall remain consistent throughout the application. |*

*| FR-0013 | Navigation shall support keyboard accessibility. |*

*| FR-0014 | Navigation shall remain functional without JavaScript whenever practical. |*

*# 13. Traceability*



*Each functional requirement shall remain traceable to:*



*- Product Goals*

*- User Personas*

*- User Journeys*

*- Architecture*

*- Design System*

*- Source Code*

*- Test Cases*



*Traceability shall be maintained throughout the project lifecycle.*



*---*



*# 14. Governance*



*New functional requirements shall receive unique identifiers.*



*Existing identifiers shall never be reused.*



*Deprecated requirements shall remain documented.*



*---*



*# 15. Compliance*



*Every implemented feature shall satisfy one or more functional requirements defined in this document.*



*Implementation without documented requirements shall be considered incomplete.*
