*# 1. Purpose*



*This document defines the long-term vision of the Francisco Sánchez Portfolio.*



*It establishes the purpose of the product, the value it delivers, the audience it serves, and the principles that guide its evolution.*



*The Product Vision serves as the highest-level reference for every product decision made throughout the project lifecycle.*



*---*



*# 2. Product Overview*



*The Francisco Sánchez Portfolio is a professional digital platform that demonstrates software engineering capabilities through real-world projects, technical documentation, architectural decisions, and production-quality implementations.*



*Rather than functioning as a traditional résumé, the portfolio serves as an engineering product designed to communicate technical competence, professional maturity, and long-term growth.*



*The portfolio itself is intended to be an example of the engineering standards it promotes.*



*---*



*# 3. Vision Statement*



*To build a portfolio that reflects engineering excellence through transparency, documentation, maintainability, and real-world software solutions, becoming a trusted reference for recruiters, engineering leaders, collaborators, and clients.*



*---*



*# 4. Product Purpose*



*The product exists to:*



*- Demonstrate engineering capabilities.*

*- Showcase complete software projects.*

*- Present architectural thinking.*

*- Document engineering decisions.*

*- Build professional credibility.*

*- Support career growth.*

*- Serve as a long-term engineering platform.*



*---*



*# 5. Target Audience*



*The portfolio is intended for:*



*- Recruiters.*

*- Engineering Managers.*

*- Technical Leads.*

*- Software Architects.*

*- CTOs.*

*- CEOs.*

*- Clients.*

*- Collaborators.*

*- Software Engineers.*

*- Students interested in engineering practices.*



*Each audience should be able to find relevant information without unnecessary complexity.*



*---*



*# 6. Product Value Proposition*



*The portfolio differentiates itself by combining:*



*- Professional documentation.*

*- Real engineering projects.*

*- Complete case studies.*

*- Architecture-first thinking.*

*- Modern frontend development.*

*- Production-ready implementations.*

*- Long-term maintainability.*



*The portfolio demonstrates engineering capability through evidence rather than claims.*



*---*



*# 7. Product Principles*



*The product shall follow these principles.*



*- Documentation First.*

*- User-Centered Design.*

*- Engineering Quality.*

*- Accessibility.*

*- Performance.*

*- Transparency.*

*- Maintainability.*

*- Scalability.*

*- Continuous Improvement.*



*These principles guide every future product decision.*



*---*



*# 8. Product Scope*



*The portfolio includes:*



*- Professional homepage.*

*- About section.*

*- Skills.*

*- Experience.*

*- Project showcase.*

*- Engineering documentation.*

*- Technical blog.*

*- Case studies.*

*- Resume.*

*- Contact section.*



*Additional features may be incorporated as the product evolves.*



*---*



*# 9. Out of Scope*



*The following are intentionally excluded from the initial product scope.*



*- Social networking features.*

*- User-generated content.*

*- Forums.*

*- Authentication for visitors.*

*- E-commerce functionality.*

*- Advertising.*

*- Subscription services.*



*Future inclusion requires formal product review.*



*---*



*# 10. Long-Term Vision*



*The portfolio should evolve into a comprehensive engineering platform capable of documenting professional growth over many years.*



*It should remain adaptable to new technologies while preserving architectural consistency and documentation quality.*



*Long-term sustainability takes precedence over rapid expansion.*



*---*



*# 11. Success Criteria*



*The product will be considered successful when it:*



*- Demonstrates engineering maturity.*

*- Clearly communicates professional capabilities.*

*- Supports technical interviews.*

*- Builds trust with recruiters and clients.*

*- Provides valuable engineering documentation.*

*- Remains maintainable over time.*



*---*



*# 12. Governance*



*The Product Vision defined herein governs all future product decisions.*



*New features, structural changes, or strategic modifications shall remain aligned with this vision unless superseded by an approved product decision.*



*---*



*# 13. Compliance*



*Every document within the Product domain shall align with the vision established in this document.*



*Significant deviations require documentation, review, and approval.*
