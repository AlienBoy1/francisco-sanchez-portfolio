*# 1. Purpose*



*This document defines the official Review Process governing the Francisco Sánchez Portfolio AI-First engineering ecosystem.*



*The Review Process establishes the standardized methodology for evaluating engineering artifacts before approval, ensuring consistency, quality, traceability, and compliance with engineering standards and governance policies.*



*Every review shall follow this process.*



*---*



*# 2. Objectives*



*The Review Process aims to:*



*- Standardize engineering reviews.*

*- Improve artifact quality.*

*- Detect defects early.*

*- Preserve architectural consistency.*

*- Ensure documentation synchronization.*

*- Enable AI-assisted reviews.*

*- Produce objective review evidence.*

*- Support engineering collaboration.*



*---*



*# 3. Review Philosophy*



*Reviews are collaborative engineering activities.*



*The objective of a review is to improve the artifact—not to evaluate the author.*



*Reviews shall remain:*



*- Constructive.*

*- Objective.*

*- Evidence-based.*

*- Respectful.*

*- Traceable.*



*Blameless engineering applies to every review.*



*---*



*# 4. Scope*



*This process applies to reviews involving:*



*- Source code.*

*- Documentation.*

*- Architecture.*

*- AI prompts.*

*- AI agents.*

*- Infrastructure.*

*- Workflows.*

*- Templates.*

*- Releases.*

*- Operational procedures.*



*Every engineering artifact requiring approval shall be reviewed.*



*---*



*# 5. Review Types*



*The ecosystem defines the following review categories:*



*- Documentation Review*

*- Code Review*

*- Architecture Review*

*- AI Review*

*- Security Review*

*- Infrastructure Review*

*- Operational Review*

*- Release Review*



*Each review type may include specialized criteria while following the same process.*



*---*



*# 6. Participants*



*Typical participants include:*



*- Artifact Author*

*- Reviewer*

*- Architecture Reviewer*

*- Project Owner*

*- AI Review Agent*

*- Documentation Agent*

*- Quality Agent*



*Responsibilities shall be clearly assigned before every review.*



*---*



*# 7. Review Lifecycle*



*```*

*Artifact Submitted*



*↓*



*Preparation*



*↓*



*Independent Review*



*↓*



*Findings*



*↓*



*Discussion*



*↓*



*Corrections*



*↓*



*Revalidation*



*↓*



*Approval*



*↓*



*Evidence Registration*

*```*



*Every review shall complete the full lifecycle.*



*---*



*# 8. Stage 1 — Artifact Submission*



*Objectives:*



*- Register artifact.*

*- Identify artifact type.*

*- Define review scope.*

*- Assign reviewers.*



*Outputs:*



*- Review Request.*



*---*



*# 9. Stage 2 — Preparation*



*Objectives:*



*- Gather supporting documentation.*

*- Verify prerequisites.*

*- Confirm Quality Gates.*

*- Review related ADRs and standards.*



*Outputs:*



*- Review Package.*



*---*



*# 10. Stage 3 — Independent Review*



*Objectives:*



*- Evaluate artifact independently.*

*- Avoid reviewer bias.*

*- Verify engineering quality.*

*- Produce objective observations.*



*Reviewers shall base conclusions on engineering evidence.*



*---*



*# 11. Stage 4 — Findings*



*Findings shall be classified as:*



*- Critical*

*- Major*

*- Minor*

*- Observation*

*- Recommendation*



*Every finding shall include:*



*- Description.*

*- Evidence.*

*- Impact.*

*- Suggested resolution.*



*---*



*# 12. Stage 5 — Discussion*



*Objectives:*



*- Clarify findings.*

*- Resolve misunderstandings.*

*- Reach technical consensus.*

*- Preserve engineering rationale.*



*Engineering arguments shall prevail over personal preference.*



*---*



*# 13. Stage 6 — Corrections*



*Objectives:*



*- Resolve accepted findings.*

*- Update documentation.*

*- Correct implementation.*

*- Synchronize affected artifacts.*



*Every correction shall remain traceable.*



*---*



*# 14. Stage 7 — Revalidation*



*Objectives:*



*- Verify corrections.*

*- Confirm finding resolution.*

*- Re-evaluate Quality Gates if necessary.*



*Only corrected findings shall be revalidated.*



*---*



*# 15. Stage 8 — Approval*



*Possible review outcomes:*



*- Approved*

*- Approved with Observations*

*- Changes Requested*

*- Rejected*



*Only Approved and Approved with Observations allow progression.*



*---*



*# 16. Stage 9 — Evidence Registration*



*Every completed review shall register:*



*- Reviewer.*

*- Review date.*

*- Findings.*

*- Resolution.*

*- Final decision.*

*- Supporting evidence.*



*Review evidence shall remain permanently traceable.*



*---*



*# 17. Review Criteria*



*Reviews shall evaluate:*



*- Correctness.*

*- Completeness.*

*- Consistency.*

*- Maintainability.*

*- Security.*

*- Architecture compliance.*

*- Documentation synchronization.*

*- Governance compliance.*



*The applicable criteria depend on the artifact type.*



*---*



*# 18. Review Independence*



*Whenever practical:*



*- Reviewers shall not review their own work.*

*- Architecture reviews shall remain independent.*

*- Security reviews shall remain independent.*

*- AI reviews shall complement—but never replace—human reviews.*



*Independent reviews increase engineering confidence.*



*---*



*# 19. AI Integration*



*AI systems may assist with:*



*- Static analysis.*

*- Documentation review.*

*- Code analysis.*

*- Prompt evaluation.*

*- Pattern detection.*

*- Risk identification.*

*- Consistency validation.*

*- Review report generation.*



*AI recommendations shall always be explainable.*



*---*



*# 20. Metrics*



*Recommended indicators include:*



*- Review coverage.*

*- Review duration.*

*- First-pass approval rate.*

*- Findings per review.*

*- Critical finding frequency.*

*- Rework rate.*

*- AI recommendation acceptance.*

*- Reviewer workload.*



*Metrics shall support process optimization.*



*---*



*# 21. Continuous Improvement*



*The Review Process shall evolve through:*



*- Retrospectives.*

*- Repository audits.*

*- Engineering feedback.*

*- AI recommendations.*

*- Operational metrics.*

*- Governance reviews.*



*Review quality shall be periodically evaluated.*



*---*



*# 22. AI Operational Contract*



*## Responsibilities*



*AI systems shall:*



*- Analyze engineering artifacts.*

*- Detect inconsistencies.*

*- Recommend improvements.*

*- Produce review reports.*

*- Preserve repository consistency.*



*---*



*## Authorized Actions*



*AI may:*



*- Review artifacts.*

*- Detect violations.*

*- Suggest improvements.*

*- Explain findings.*

*- Produce summaries.*



*---*



*## Restricted Actions*



*AI shall not:*



*- Approve reviews autonomously.*

*- Reject artifacts autonomously.*

*- Override reviewer decisions.*

*- Ignore governance policies.*



*---*



*## Success Criteria*



*The Review Process is successful when:*



*- Engineering quality improves.*

*- Defects are detected early.*

*- Reviews remain objective.*

*- Documentation stays synchronized.*

*- Architecture consistency increases.*

*- AI accelerates review efficiency without reducing quality.*



*---*



*## Failure Conditions*



*The Review Process fails when:*



*- Reviews become subjective.*

*- Findings lack evidence.*

*- Critical issues reach production.*

*- Documentation diverges from implementation.*

*- Review evidence is lost.*



*---*



*## Escalation*



*Any review involving architecture, security, governance, AI systems, production infrastructure, or repository standards that results in unresolved critical findings shall be escalated to the Project Owner before approval.*



*---*



*# 23. Governance*



*The Review Process governs every formal engineering review executed within the AI-First engineering ecosystem.*



*All future review procedures, Pull Request policies, AI review agents, auditing systems, CI/CD validation workflows, and engineering governance mechanisms shall comply with this process.*
