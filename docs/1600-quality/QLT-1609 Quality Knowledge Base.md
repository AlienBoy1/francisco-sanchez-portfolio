*# 1. Purpose*



*This document defines the official Quality Knowledge Base governing the Francisco Sánchez Portfolio AI-First engineering ecosystem.*



*The Quality Knowledge Base establishes the standardized approach for capturing, organizing, preserving, maintaining, and reusing quality-related knowledge generated throughout the engineering lifecycle.*



*Engineering knowledge is a strategic organizational asset.*



*Knowledge shall never depend on individuals.*



*---*



*# 2. Objectives*



*The Quality Knowledge Base aims to:*



*- Preserve engineering knowledge.*

*- Prevent repeated mistakes.*

*- Accelerate engineering decisions.*

*- Improve AI collaboration.*

*- Support continuous learning.*

*- Strengthen governance.*

*- Reduce organizational knowledge loss.*

*- Enable long-term engineering evolution.*



*---*



*# 3. Knowledge Philosophy*



*Knowledge shall be:*



*- Explicit.*

*- Documented.*

*- Traceable.*

*- Version controlled.*

*- Searchable.*

*- Reusable.*

*- Continuously maintained.*



*Knowledge not documented shall be considered unavailable.*



*---*



*# 4. Scope*



*The Quality Knowledge Base includes knowledge related to:*



*- Engineering Standards.*

*- Architecture.*

*- Documentation.*

*- Quality.*

*- Governance.*

*- Reviews.*

*- Audits.*

*- AI Collaboration.*

*- Operational Experience.*

*- Technical Debt.*

*- Continuous Improvement.*

*- Lessons Learned.*



*---*



*# 5. Knowledge Sources*



*Knowledge may originate from:*



*- Engineering Reviews.*

*- Audits.*

*- Architecture Decisions (ADRs).*

*- Incident Reports.*

*- Postmortems.*

*- Quality Metrics.*

*- AI Recommendations.*

*- Retrospectives.*

*- Continuous Improvement Initiatives.*

*- Repository Evolution.*



*Every knowledge item shall reference its origin.*



*---*



*# 6. Knowledge Categories*



*The ecosystem defines the following categories:*



*- Standards*

*- Best Practices*

*- Lessons Learned*

*- Known Risks*

*- Architectural Knowledge*

*- Operational Knowledge*

*- AI Knowledge*

*- Security Knowledge*

*- Governance Knowledge*

*- Repository Knowledge*



*Categories shall remain consistently classified.*



*---*



*# 7. Knowledge Lifecycle*



*```*

*Knowledge Creation*



*↓*



*Validation*



*↓*



*Classification*



*↓*



*Publication*



*↓*



*Reuse*



*↓*



*Review*



*↓*



*Update*



*↓*

*```*



*Every knowledge artifact shall follow this lifecycle.*



*---*



*# 8. Knowledge Creation*



*Knowledge shall be created whenever:*



*- A significant engineering decision is made.*

*- A recurring issue is solved.*

*- An important lesson is learned.*

*- A new engineering pattern is adopted.*

*- A governance improvement is approved.*

*- An AI recommendation becomes institutionalized.*



*Knowledge creation is a continuous responsibility.*



*---*



*# 9. Knowledge Validation*



*Before publication, knowledge shall be validated for:*



*- Accuracy.*

*- Relevance.*

*- Consistency.*

*- Traceability.*

*- Governance compliance.*



*Only validated knowledge shall become part of the official repository.*



*---*



*# 10. Knowledge Classification*



*Each knowledge artifact shall include:*



*- Identifier.*

*- Title.*

*- Category.*

*- Source.*

*- Related documents.*

*- Version.*

*- Status.*

*- Owner.*

*- Creation date.*

*- Last review date.*



*Consistent metadata enables long-term discoverability.*



*---*



*# 11. Knowledge Reuse*



*Engineering teams and AI systems shall prioritize existing knowledge before creating new guidance.*



*Knowledge reuse shall reduce:*



*- Duplicate solutions.*

*- Inconsistent decisions.*

*- Repeated mistakes.*

*- Documentation fragmentation.*



*---*



*# 12. Knowledge Maintenance*



*Knowledge shall be periodically reviewed to ensure:*



*- Continued accuracy.*

*- Technical relevance.*

*- Alignment with governance.*

*- Compatibility with current standards.*



*Outdated knowledge shall be revised or archived.*



*---*



*# 13. Lessons Learned*



*Lessons Learned shall document:*



*- Situation.*

*- Root cause.*

*- Resolution.*

*- Preventive actions.*

*- Related standards.*

*- Long-term recommendations.*



*Lessons Learned shall feed Continuous Quality Improvement.*



*---*



*# 14. AI Integration*



*AI systems may:*



*- Retrieve knowledge.*

*- Recommend relevant standards.*

*- Detect duplicate content.*

*- Suggest documentation updates.*

*- Link related artifacts.*

*- Identify knowledge gaps.*



*AI shall preserve repository consistency.*



*---*



*# 15. Searchability*



*Knowledge shall be:*



*- Easily searchable.*

*- Properly indexed.*

*- Cross-referenced.*

*- Version controlled.*

*- Linked to related artifacts.*



*Information retrieval shall require minimal effort.*



*---*



*# 16. Organizational Learning*



*The Quality Knowledge Base shall support:*



*- Onboarding.*

*- Engineering training.*

*- AI context loading.*

*- Architecture evolution.*

*- Governance consistency.*

*- Decision continuity.*



*Organizational learning shall remain institutional.*



*---*



*# 17. AI Operational Contract*



*## Responsibilities*



*AI systems shall:*



*- Retrieve knowledge.*

*- Recommend existing guidance.*

*- Detect duplication.*

*- Identify inconsistencies.*

*- Preserve repository organization.*



*---*



*## Authorized Actions*



*AI may:*



*- Search documentation.*

*- Suggest cross references.*

*- Recommend updates.*

*- Detect obsolete knowledge.*

*- Generate knowledge summaries.*



*---*



*## Restricted Actions*



*AI shall not:*



*- Publish knowledge autonomously.*

*- Remove historical knowledge.*

*- Modify governance standards.*

*- Override human validation.*



*---*



*## Success Criteria*



*The Quality Knowledge Base is successful when:*



*- Knowledge is easily discoverable.*

*- Engineering decisions become faster.*

*- Duplicate work decreases.*

*- Lessons Learned are reused.*

*- AI consistently retrieves relevant knowledge.*

*- Organizational memory grows over time.*



*---*



*## Failure Conditions*



*The Quality Knowledge Base fails when:*



*- Knowledge becomes fragmented.*

*- Documentation is duplicated.*

*- Lessons are repeatedly forgotten.*

*- Historical decisions become unrecoverable.*

*- AI retrieves inconsistent information.*



*---*



*## Escalation*



*Critical knowledge loss, repository inconsistency, missing governance documentation, broken architectural traceability, or loss of historical engineering decisions shall require immediate investigation by the Project Owner.*



*---*



*# 18. Governance*



*The Quality Knowledge Base governs the preservation and evolution of engineering knowledge throughout the AI-First engineering ecosystem.*



*All future repositories, documentation systems, AI memory mechanisms, knowledge management initiatives, engineering onboarding processes, and organizational learning programs shall comply with this standard.*



*Knowledge is a permanent engineering asset.*
