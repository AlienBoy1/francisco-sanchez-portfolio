*# 1. Purpose*



*This document defines the engineering principles, governance model, and architectural foundation for all reusable templates within the Francisco Sánchez Portfolio AI-First engineering ecosystem.*



*Templates provide standardized structures that ensure consistency, quality, traceability, automation readiness, and long-term maintainability across every engineering artifact produced by humans and AI Agents.*



*Every official template shall comply with the principles defined herein.*



*---*



*# 2. Objectives*



*The Template Foundation aims to:*



*- Standardize engineering artifacts.*

*- Reduce documentation variability.*

*- Increase development consistency.*

*- Improve AI automation.*

*- Preserve governance.*

*- Accelerate project initialization.*

*- Promote organizational knowledge reuse.*

*- Support long-term scalability.*



*---*



*# 3. Template Philosophy*



*Templates are reusable engineering assets.*



*They define structure—not content.*



*Every template establishes a consistent framework while allowing engineering teams and AI Agents to populate it with project-specific information.*



*Templates are intended to reduce cognitive load, improve quality, and enable predictable automation.*



*---*



*# 4. Engineering Principles*



*Every official template shall be:*



*- Standardized.*

*- Modular.*

*- Reusable.*

*- Extensible.*

*- Version controlled.*

*- Documented.*

*- Governed.*

*- AI-friendly.*



*Templates shall prioritize clarity over complexity.*



*---*



*# 5. Template Architecture*



*Every template shall include, where applicable:*



*- Metadata.*

*- Purpose.*

*- Scope.*

*- Responsibilities.*

*- Required Sections.*

*- References.*

*- Related Documents.*

*- Revision History.*

*- Approval.*



*Templates may extend this structure according to their domain.*



*---*



*# 6. Template Categories*



*Official templates are grouped into the following categories:*



*- Documentation Templates*

*- Architecture Templates*

*- Development Templates*

*- Governance Templates*

*- AI Templates*

*- Project Templates*

*- Operational Templates*



*New categories require governance approval.*



*---*



*# 7. Naming Convention*



*Every template shall follow the format:*



*```*

*PREFIX-ID Title.md*

*```*



*Example:*



*```*

*TMP-1406 AI Prompt Template.md*

*```*



*Identifiers shall remain unique.*



*---*



*# 8. Versioning*



*Templates shall follow Semantic Versioning.*



*```*

*MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH*

*```*



*Version changes shall be documented in the Revision History section.*



*---*



*# 9. Governance*



*Official templates shall:*



*- Be reviewed before approval.*

*- Remain version controlled.*

*- Follow engineering standards.*

*- Preserve backward compatibility whenever practical.*

*- Be maintained throughout their lifecycle.*



*Only approved templates may be used in production engineering workflows.*



*---*



*# 10. AI Compatibility*



*Templates shall be designed for both human engineers and AI Agents.*



*Every template shall:*



*- Minimize ambiguity.*

*- Use deterministic structures.*

*- Be machine-readable.*

*- Support automation.*

*- Enable predictable content generation.*



*---*



*# 11. Reusability*



*Templates shall maximize reuse by:*



*- Avoiding project-specific assumptions.*

*- Separating structure from implementation.*

*- Supporting inheritance where appropriate.*

*- Remaining technology agnostic whenever possible.*



*---*



*# 12. Lifecycle*



*Templates progress through the following lifecycle:*



*```*

*Draft*

&#x20;  *│*

*Review*

&#x20;  *│*

*Approved*

&#x20;  *│*

*Published*

&#x20;  *│*

*Maintained*

&#x20;  *│*

*Versioned*

&#x20;  *│*

*Deprecated*

&#x20;  *│*

*Retired*

*```*



*Every lifecycle transition shall be documented.*



*---*



*# 13. Quality Requirements*



*Every template shall satisfy:*



*- Structural consistency.*

*- Documentation completeness.*

*- AI readability.*

*- Human readability.*

*- Governance compliance.*

*- Version control.*

*- Maintainability.*



*Templates shall undergo periodic reviews.*



*---*



*# 14. AI Operational Contract*



*## Responsibilities*



*Every AI Agent shall:*



*- Use approved templates.*

*- Preserve template structure.*

*- Populate only applicable sections.*

*- Respect template versioning.*

*- Recommend improvements when appropriate.*



*---*



*## Authorized Actions*



*Agents may:*



*- Instantiate templates.*

*- Extend template content.*

*- Recommend new template types.*

*- Suggest structural improvements.*

*- Validate template compliance.*



*---*



*## Restricted Actions*



*Agents shall not:*



*- Modify approved templates without versioning.*

*- Remove mandatory sections.*

*- Ignore governance requirements.*

*- Create unofficial production templates.*

*- Break template compatibility without approval.*



*---*



*## Success Criteria*



*The Template Foundation is successful when:*



*- Engineering artifacts become structurally consistent.*

*- AI-generated outputs become predictable.*

*- Documentation quality improves.*

*- Automation complexity decreases.*

*- Organizational knowledge becomes reusable.*



*---*



*## Failure Conditions*



*The Template Foundation fails when:*



*- Multiple incompatible template formats emerge.*

*- AI outputs become inconsistent.*

*- Governance is bypassed.*

*- Template duplication increases.*

*- Engineering artifacts lose structural consistency.*



*---*



*## Escalation*



*Structural modifications affecting official templates shall be approved by the Project Owner before adoption.*



*---*



*# 15. Governance*



*The Template Foundation governs every reusable engineering template within the AI-First engineering ecosystem.*



*Future documentation generators, AI orchestration platforms, engineering assistants, project generators, and automation systems shall remain compatible with the principles established herein.*
