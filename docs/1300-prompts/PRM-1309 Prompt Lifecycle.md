*# 1. Purpose*



*This document defines the standardized lifecycle governing every production prompt within the Francisco Sánchez Portfolio AI-First engineering ecosystem.*



*The objective is to ensure that prompts are created, reviewed, validated, deployed, maintained, evolved, and retired using a controlled engineering process that guarantees quality, security, traceability, and governance.*



*Every production prompt shall follow the lifecycle defined herein.*



*---*



*# 2. Objectives*



*The Prompt Lifecycle framework aims to:*



*- Standardize prompt evolution.*

*- Improve engineering quality.*

*- Preserve governance.*

*- Support continuous improvement.*

*- Maintain prompt traceability.*

*- Reduce operational risk.*

*- Enable sustainable maintenance.*

*- Promote long-term knowledge preservation.*



*---*



*# 3. Lifecycle Philosophy*



*Prompts are living engineering assets.*



*Every prompt has a beginning, an operational life, continuous evolution, and an eventual retirement.*



*Lifecycle management ensures that prompts remain valuable throughout their existence.*



*---*



*# 4. Engineering Principles*



*Prompt lifecycle management shall be:*



*- Structured.*

*- Repeatable.*

*- Traceable.*

*- Governed.*

*- Version controlled.*

*- Measurable.*

*- Secure.*

*- Continuously reviewed.*



*---*

*---*



*# 6. Idea Stage*



*The lifecycle begins with identifying:*



*- Business need.*

*- Engineering opportunity.*

*- Operational improvement.*

*- Automation requirement.*

*- AI capability.*



*Every new prompt shall have a documented purpose.*



*---*



*# 7. Design Stage*



*Prompt design shall define:*



*- Objectives.*

*- Architecture.*

*- Constraints.*

*- Context requirements.*

*- Expected outputs.*

*- Dependencies.*



*Design shall comply with Prompt Architecture standards.*



*---*



*# 8. Development Stage*



*Development includes:*



*- Prompt construction.*

*- Template selection.*

*- Metadata creation.*

*- Documentation.*

*- Initial testing.*



*Development shall follow Prompt Engineering standards.*



*---*



*# 9. Validation Stage*



*Every prompt shall undergo:*



*- Functional validation.*

*- Architecture review.*

*- Security validation.*

*- Governance verification.*

*- Quality assessment.*



*Validation shall follow PRM-1303.*



*---*



*# 10. Approval Stage*



*Approved prompts shall receive:*



*- Official identifier.*

*- Version assignment.*

*- Ownership.*

*- Lifecycle status.*

*- Registration within the Prompt Library.*



*Approval authorizes production use.*



*---*



*# 11. Production Stage*



*Production prompts shall:*



*- Be version controlled.*

*- Remain documented.*

*- Preserve metadata.*

*- Respect governance.*

*- Support monitoring.*



*Production is not the final lifecycle stage.*



*---*



*# 12. Monitoring Stage*



*Operational monitoring shall evaluate:*



*- Prompt performance.*

*- Output quality.*

*- User feedback.*

*- Hallucination frequency.*

*- Governance compliance.*

*- Security incidents.*



*Monitoring provides evidence for future improvements.*



*---*



*# 13. Optimization Stage*



*Optimization shall improve:*



*- Readability.*

*- Maintainability.*

*- Reuse.*

*- Performance.*

*- Reliability.*

*- AI effectiveness.*



*Optimization shall preserve engineering integrity.*



*---*



*# 14. Version Upgrade*



*Prompt evolution shall follow Semantic Versioning.*



*Version upgrades may introduce:*



*- Improvements.*

*- New capabilities.*

*- Architectural changes.*

*- Security enhancements.*

*- Compatibility updates.*



*Every version shall preserve historical traceability.*



*---*



*# 15. Deprecation*



*Prompts shall be deprecated when:*



*- Superior alternatives exist.*

*- Technology evolves.*

*- Engineering standards change.*

*- Maintenance becomes impractical.*

*- Operational value decreases.*



*Deprecated prompts may remain temporarily available.*



*---*



*# 16. Retirement*



*Retired prompts:*



*- Shall not be used in production.*

*- Shall remain archived.*

*- Shall preserve historical documentation.*

*- Shall retain revision history.*

*- Shall remain traceable.*



*Retirement preserves organizational knowledge.*



*---*



*# 17. Continuous Improvement*



*Lifecycle management is iterative.*



*Every operational prompt shall periodically return to:*



*- Monitoring.*

*- Optimization.*

*- Validation.*

*- Versioning.*



*Continuous improvement never ends.*



*---*



*# 18. AI Operational Contract*



*## Responsibilities*



*Every AI Agent shall:*



*- Respect lifecycle stages.*

*- Preserve traceability.*

*- Recommend lifecycle transitions.*

*- Support optimization.*

*- Maintain governance compliance.*



*---*



*## Authorized Actions*



*Agents may:*



*- Create lifecycle documentation.*

*- Recommend promotions.*

*- Suggest optimizations.*

*- Initiate deprecation reviews.*

*- Produce lifecycle reports.*



*---*



*## Restricted Actions*



*Agents shall not:*



*- Skip lifecycle stages.*

*- Deploy unapproved prompts.*

*- Retire prompts without governance approval.*

*- Remove lifecycle history.*

*- Bypass validation.*



*---*



*## Success Criteria*



*Prompt Lifecycle is successful when:*



*- Every prompt follows the defined lifecycle.*

*- Prompt quality improves continuously.*

*- Governance remains intact.*

*- Historical traceability is preserved.*

*- Prompt evolution remains sustainable.*



*---*



*## Failure Conditions*



*Prompt Lifecycle fails when:*



*- Lifecycle stages are skipped.*

*- Prompt history is lost.*

*- Governance is bypassed.*

*- Deprecated prompts remain unmanaged.*

*- Continuous improvement ceases.*



*---*



*## Escalation*



*Major lifecycle transitions affecting production prompts shall be approved by the Project Owner.*



*---*



*# 19. Governance*



*The Prompt Lifecycle framework governs the complete operational lifecycle of every production prompt within the AI-First engineering ecosystem.*



*Future prompt registries, lifecycle managers, orchestration platforms, AI engineering suites, and governance systems shall remain compatible with the principles established herein.*
